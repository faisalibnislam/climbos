**Diagnosis:** Acquisition volume is unproven. The selected assumption-based scenarios total ≈7.5 paid customers (range 2.5–20) in 90 days; the North Star is ≈8. Measured results will show whether the plays can sustain growth.

**North Star:** New paid customers from selected plays: ≈8 new paid customers by day 90; scenario estimate.

**Risks:** LTV model relies on unverified plan mix, margin, and churn. · Creator posts may not generate tagged visits or paid upgrades. · Current paid baseline and acquisition sources are unknown. · Selected plays may not achieve the ≈8-customer day-90 target.
