**Audiences**

## Typeform payers who feel they overpay

**Name:** Typeform payers who feel they overpay

**Description:** Freelancers, coaches and small teams (1–3 seats) on a paid Typeform plan. Trigger: a renewal bill or hitting a plan limit. Evidence: 5 of 8 Typeform-customer snippets complain about price, and 2 Tally reviews name Typeform's price as the reason they switched.

**Confidence:** medium

## Free-tier users who hit a response cap

**Name:** Free-tier users who hit a response cap

**Description:** Teams on capped free plans (Typeform: 10 responses a month; Fillout: 1,000; Jotform: low limits, per reviewers) whose forms get 100+ responses a month (assumption threshold). Formkit Free has unlimited forms, responses and members.

**Confidence:** medium

## People who already work in Claude or ChatGPT

**Name:** People who already work in Claude or ChatGPT

**Description:** Users who want to build and publish forms from a chat window, which Formkit supports. It is unverified whether rivals offer this through MCP.

**Confidence:** low

## Event organisers, trainers and coaches taking payments

**Name:** Event organisers, trainers and coaches taking payments

**Description:** Payments go through their own Stripe account with no Formkit cut. There is no customer evidence yet that they choose a form tool for this.

**Confidence:** low

## Disqualified: 5+ seat teams on monthly billing, and anyone needing a free custom domain

**Name:** Disqualified: 5+ seat teams on monthly billing, and anyone needing a free custom domain

**Description:** At 5 seats on monthly billing, Pro costs $30, more than Tally Pro at about $29. Custom domains are Pro-only. Don't target either group with ads.

**Confidence:** medium

**Swot**

**Strengths**

- Free: unlimited forms, responses and members.

- Builds and publishes forms inside Claude and ChatGPT.

- Per-question drop-off, kept partial answers and 30-day version history on Free (own compare page).

- $6 a seat entry price; Stripe payments with no Formkit cut.

- One-click cancel, no survey.

**Weaknesses**

- Name collision: searching 'Formkit' returns the unrelated FormKit Vue.js library.

- Zero public reviews found, so no social proof.

- Per-seat pricing costs more than flat-priced rivals at 3–5 seats.

- Small Pro AI allowance on responses: 20 a seat a month.

**Opportunities**

- Typeform price-switchers: price is the top theme, 8 of 18 snippets.

- Free-plan cap complaints: 5 of 18 snippets across 3 rival pools.

- Tally users asking for a statistics graph and version history.

- An in-chat building angle few rivals visibly claim (unverified).

**Threats**

- Tally already offers free unlimited submissions.

- Fillout already has AI generation; rivals could add MCP quickly.

- Google Forms remains the free default.

- Brand-search confusion sends clicks to the Vue library.
