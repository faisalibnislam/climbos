## Post 1: A form should adapt to the answer

**Angle and audience:** Use case for sales and service teams qualifying enquiries.

### Post text

A lead qualification form shouldn’t ask every person the same follow-up questions.

If someone asks about branding, ask about branding. If they need a website, take them down that path instead.

That’s what conditional logic is for: show relevant questions, skip what doesn’t apply, and let people see a form that responds to their answers.

In Formkit, you can describe a rule in plain words, see the paths on a logic map, and test them before you publish.

A good form doesn’t just collect information. It guides the conversation.

## Post 3: AI can draft the form. You decide when it goes live.

**Angle and audience:** Addresses concerns about handing form creation and publishing over to AI; relevant to teams that want a faster first draft with human review.

### Post text

“AI builds the form” shouldn’t mean “AI decides what we publish.”

In Formkit, describe the form you need and Ask Formkit drafts the questions, pages and logic. You can ask for changes, review the draft, and choose when to publish. Nothing goes live until you publish it.

For example, start with a brief for a client intake form, review the questions, adjust the flow, then publish when it’s ready.

AI can help with the first pass. The final call stays with you.
