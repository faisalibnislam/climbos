# Formkit: 90-Day Growth Plan

## Recommendation

Aim for **8 net-new paying company accounts by day 90**. This is a planning target, not a forecast backed by Formkit's current traffic or customer data; those baselines were not available. Recalculate after week one, when you know the number of active free workspaces and their activation rates.

Formkit's site lists a free plan, Pro at $6 per seat per month, and Business at $19 per seat per month. It highlights AI-built forms, conditional logic, agency and sales use cases, and Claude/ChatGPT support. The plan focuses on turning those strengths into paid workspaces. ([Pricing](https://formkit.app/pricing) · [Use cases](https://formkit.app/use-cases) · [AI assistants](https://formkit.app/features/ai-assistants))

The surveyed companies' typical revenue per customer was much higher than Formkit's listed per-seat prices, so treat these benchmarks as directional. ([ChartMogul, 2026 SaaS Conversion Report](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report-2/))

If Formkit has fewer eligible workspaces or attracts less traffic, the result falls accordingly. This is a planning model, not a claim about Formkit's current audience.

## North Star and channels

**North Star:** net-new paid company accounts still paying 30 days after first payment. **Day-90 target: 8.** Track seats and MRR alongside it, since plans are priced per seat.

Channel priority:

1. **Product activation and paid-feature upgrade moments** — closest to existing product value; no ad spend required.
2. **Search pages and templates for specific jobs** — make high-intent use cases easier to find and try.
3. **Claude/ChatGPT and developer-community discovery** — a differentiated route tied to a feature Formkit already offers.
4. **Small Google Search test, only after tracking works and a likely CAC ceiling is known.**

**Not now:** broad LinkedIn or Meta ads, mass email, influencer spend, and a launch-day spike as the main strategy. With a $600 monthly ceiling and low per-seat pricing, those could consume budget before there's evidence of paid conversion. WordStream's 2025 benchmark reports a $5.26 average CPC across U.S. search campaigns, not a Formkit- or SaaS-specific rate; even a small campaign may buy too few clicks to judge confidently. ([WordStream Google Ads benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks))

## Phases

- **Days 1–7 — establish the baseline:** instrument the funnel, count eligible free workspaces, check which use cases bring signups, and define activation.
- **Days 8–30 — ship the first tests:** launch the sales qualification template page and the free-workspace upgrade moment; verify the AI-assistant workflow.
- **Days 31–60 — learn from real cohorts:** compare signup, activation, and paid conversion by source. Publish the AI workflow guide. Consider a small exact-intent search test only if tracking and the CAC ceiling are in place.
- **Days 61–90 — concentrate effort:** improve or expand the play producing activated workspaces and paid accounts; stop the others if they miss their kill rules.

Keep the owner workload to roughly **3–4 hours per week**—an operating assumption: one hour on metrics and customer feedback, one to two hours on the pages or demo, and one hour reviewing conversion and making changes.

## Week-one checklist

- [ ] Record current site visits, free signups, active workspaces, paid workspaces, seats, and cancellations.
- [ ] Track `signup → form created → form published → first response → upgrade → payment → 30-day retention`.
- [ ] Add UTMs to every new page, guide, and campaign link.
- [ ] Define an eligible free workspace and an activation event before writing upgrade messaging.
- [ ] Choose one initial audience: **agencies/freelancers creating client intake forms** or **sales teams qualifying leads**. Don't split effort across every listed use case yet.
- [ ] Build and test one template with the intended audience before publishing it.
- [ ] Check that the analytics showcase on the site is clearly illustrative: it displays figures such as 12,483 views. Don't present those as Formkit customer results unless they're verified.
- [ ] Review any upgrade email or public material before it is sent or published.

## Ad budget

Treat **$600 per month as a ceiling**; there is no need to spend it all.

| Monthly cap | Planned spend |
|---|---:|
| Google Search, after the tracking and CAC checks | Up to $150 |
| Retargeting and broad paid social | $0 for now |
| Uncommitted reserve | $450 |

For month one, spend **$0** on ads. In months two and three, release up to $150 for a tightly scoped search test only if you can measure paid accounts back to the click and have a CAC limit based on actual seat count and retention. Otherwise, leave that budget unspent.

## Measurement

Use one weekly funnel report, split by first-touch source and use case:

- Qualified visits → free signups
- Signups → published forms → first responses
- Activated free workspaces → paid workspaces
- Paid workspaces, paid seats, MRR, and 30-day retention
- Spend and cost per retained paid workspace for any ad test

The key unknowns are Formkit's current free-workspace volume, activation rate, traffic, seat count per paid company, and retention. Use week one to replace the scenario inputs with actuals; the product's low per-seat price makes those figures essential before increasing ad spend.
