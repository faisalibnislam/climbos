# Formkit outbound prospect research

**Research date:** October 18, 2026  
**Goal:** Find 10 real companies with a current public signal that they run forms for events, training, or program enrollment.

## Ideal customer profile

Organizations that regularly register people for events, training, or applications; need conditional questions, team/group registration, payment, or a clearer view of form completion; and have a public upcoming program or other active form need. Disqualify organizations with no current form-driven program, no evidence of recurring enrollment, or an explicit no-solicitation rule. Buying triggers include a dated upcoming event, a new program, a registration deadline, or a complex registration flow. The likely decision-maker is the event, education, program, or enrollment lead; for small businesses, the founder or CEO.

**Product basis:** Formkit describes its product and features at [formkit.app/features](https://formkit.app/features); current plans are at [formkit.app/pricing](https://formkit.app/pricing). Product details and prices can change and should be checked before sending.

**Scoring:** Fit and timing scores are directional estimates based on public evidence, not verified buying intent. Size is marked “not stated” when the cited material did not show it.

### Exclusion and outreach checks

- **MBX Events** was excluded because its membership terms include an explicit no-spam provision. Do not cold-email it without a separate applicable basis or prior permission.
- The list favors organizations with a public business contact route. Before sending, check the recipient and applicable jurisdiction rules. For UK recipients, confirm whether the address belongs to a corporate subscriber or an individual subscriber and follow the relevant rules.
- U.S. commercial email must follow CAN-SPAM, including accurate sender details, a valid postal address, and a functioning opt-out. Add the business footer and opt-out language before sending.
- These are prospects, not confirmed buyers. Verify each event date, contact, and email on the linked page immediately before outreach. First lines below are based only on the public evidence described above.

## Cold email sequence

Send on business days 0, 3, 7, and 14. Insert each personal first line at `{{firstLine}}`. Add the appropriate greeting, sender footer, postal address, and opt-out language before sending.

### Day 0 — Subject: Next event form

`{{firstLine}}` Formkit turns a short brief into an editable form, adds answer-based paths, and shows where people stop. Pro adds payments, custom domains, and connections; the free plan includes unlimited forms, responses, and members. Pro is $6 per seat monthly. Would you consider trying Formkit for one upcoming event signup or application flow?

### Day 3 — Subject: Form paths

One low-risk test is to build a parallel form for a single upcoming event, leaving your current registration route alone. Formkit’s logic map lets you review and test every path before publishing. If that isn’t on your roadmap, just tell me and I’ll close this out.

### Day 7 — Subject: Drop-off data

Formkit can show views, starts, completions, average time, and the question where people drop off. That may help distinguish a form issue from low interest in an event. If your current setup already answers that, there’s no need to change it.

### Day 14 — Subject: One last idea

One more option: Pro supports payment collection, with payments going to your own Stripe account. Formkit also offers response exports and version history. If a future form is a fit, the free plan is available at https://formkit.app.
