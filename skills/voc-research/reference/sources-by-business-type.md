## Reference: VoC sources by business type

| Business type | Highest-signal public sources | Where the trade-offs hide | Query shapes that work |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | G2, Capterra, TrustRadius ("likes / dislikes / problems solved" fields); app-marketplace reviews (Shopify, HubSpot, Slack); Reddit (r/SaaS plus role subs such as r/sysadmin, r/marketing, r/accounting); Hacker News; LinkedIn comments | "Dislikes" fields; reviews that say "switched from" or "moved to"; 3-star reviews | `"[competitor] alternative"`, `"switched from [competitor]"`, `site:reddit.com "[competitor]" pricing`, `"[competitor] vs [competitor]"` |
| B2C app | App Store and Google Play reviews (2–3 star especially), Reddit, TikTok and YouTube comments, Trustpilot | "Would be 5 stars if…", reviews after an update or price change | `"[app] review" reddit`, `"[app] subscription" cancel`, `"[app] alternative"` |
| Local service | Google Business Profile reviews (own plus top 5 map-pack competitors), Yelp, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, Checkatrade / Angi / Houzz, local subreddits | 1–3 star reviews and owner responses; recommendation requests in local groups | `"[service] [city]" reviews`, `site:reddit.com r/[city] [service] recommend`, `"[competitor name] [city]" complaints` |
| Ecommerce / DTC | Amazon reviews of competing products (3-star reviews name the trade-off), Trustpilot, Reddit product subs, TikTok comments, own site reviews | 3-star reviews; "returned it because"; comparisons with a named brand | `"[product type]" "returned it"`, `site:reddit.com best [product type] for [use]`, `"[brand] vs [brand]"` |
| Agency / consultant | Clutch, Google reviews, LinkedIn recommendations, Reddit threads complaining about agencies, case studies on competitor sites | Complaint threads ("fired our agency because"), what case studies emphasise | `site:reddit.com "fired our agency"`, `"[service] agency" "never again"`, `"[competitor]" clutch reviews` |
| Creator / coach | YouTube and podcast comments, Reddit, course-platform reviews, testimonials on competitor sales pages, X/Threads replies | "Is [course] worth it" threads; refund stories | `"is [course/creator] worth it"`, `"[program]" refund`, `site:reddit.com [topic] course recommend` |

**Why Reddit matters twice:** Reddit is among the most-cited domains across AI answer engines in 2026 cross-platform studies, so what Reddit says about a category increasingly shapes what AI assistants say about it. Mining it is research and reputation monitoring at once.

**Coding cheat-sheet (four forces)**

| Force | What it sounds like | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| Push | Pain with the current way; a breaking point | "Third time the old guy didn't show up" |
| Pull | What attracts them to the new option | "Finally I can see which jobs made money" |
| Anxiety | Fear about buying or switching | "Worried I'd lose two years of data moving over" |
| Habit | Comfort with the status quo | "Honestly the spreadsheet works fine for now" |

Push plus Pull must outweigh Anxiety plus Habit for a switch to happen. Anxiety and Habit items feed the objection list; Push items feed hooks and problem copy; Pull items feed value pillars and headlines.
