## Reference: lifecycle by business type

| Type | Value moment (activation) | Repeat or renewal driver | First flows to build | Key metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2B SaaS** | First real job done in the product (first invoice sent, first report shared) | Habit plus team adoption; renewal | Behaviour-triggered onboarding to the activation event; trial-to-paid nudges; failed-payment recovery; at-risk check-in when usage drops | Activation rate; trial→paid; logo churn |
| **B2C app** | The core action in the first day | Habit loop; streaks; content freshness | Day-0/1 onboarding to the core action (push only with permission); D7 re-engagement; subscription renewal notices | D1/D7/D30 retention |
| **Local service** | Job completed, problem gone | Maintenance cycle (annual service, seasonal checks), next need | Post-job thank-you plus review ask; maintenance or rebooking reminder timed to the service interval; referral ask after a positive review | Rebooking rate; new reviews per week |
| **Ecommerce / DTC** | Product delivered and used | Use-up or replenishment cycle; new collections | Welcome (with consent); post-purchase (delivery, how to use, then cross-sell after use); replenishment reminder before typical use-up; review ask after a use window; win-back | Second-purchase rate; 60/90-day repeat; contribution margin per repeat order |
| **Agency / consultant** | First visible result for the client | Ongoing results; renewal; new projects | Kick-off onboarding; results check-ins; case-study and testimonial ask after a win; renewal conversation ahead of term end; alumni nurture | Retainer length; renewal rate; referrals received |
| **Creator / coach** | First lesson finished or first win | Progress, community, next offer | Welcome sequence; consumption nudges (finish lesson 1); low-ticket → high-ticket path; testimonial ask after a result | Completion rate; low→high-ticket conversion |

**Welcome / nurture sequence pattern** (lead from a free-sample offer; adapt timings to the business):

| # | Timing | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediately | Deliver and set expectations; ask one question |
| 2 | Day after the sample or first use | Ask for their reaction; make the first order easy (pre-filled where possible) |
| 3 | +3 days | Handle the main objection from VoC |
| 4 | +7 days | Proof: a similar customer's story, with permission |
| 5 | +14 days | An offer with a real deadline, only if one exists |
| 6 | +30 days, no order | "Should I stop sending these?" with preference options |

Exit immediately on purchase. Branch on clicks. Automated flows tend to outperform broadcasts on clicks (Klaviyo data, cited secondhand, puts automated flows around a 5.6% average click rate; treat as directional).
