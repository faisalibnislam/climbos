Fictional business: **Hearth & Hound**, a dog-walking and home-visit service in south Manchester with 6 walkers. Confirmed facts: max 4 dogs per walk, GPS photo update after every walk, same walker each week, £16 per 1-hour walk. Counts and URLs are invented for illustration.

### Good (excerpt)

> **Alternatives:** (1) a neighbour or teenager paid cash; (2) app marketplaces with a different walker each time; (3) doggy daycare. Evidence: 12 of 58 competitor reviews mention "different walker every week"; r/manchester thread "walker recommendations?" ([url]): 5 of 19 replies warn about "pack walks of 10+ dogs".
>
> **Unique attributes → value:** max 4 dogs → your dog gets attention, not crowd control → "she comes home tired, not stressed" (own review, [url]). Same walker → the dog trusts one person and the key stays with one person → less anxiety about access to the home.
>
> **Segment:** dual-working households in Didsbury/Chorlton with an anxious or reactive dog; trigger: return to office or a bad experience with a pack walker. Disqualify: owners wanting the cheapest per-walk price, dogs needing 1:1 behavioural training.
>
> **Frame and style:** "small-group dog walking for nervous dogs" (big fish, small pond). Rejected: "premium pet care" (vague, sets a price expectation we can't prove we deserve).
>
> **Headline option:** "The same walker every week, and never more than 4 dogs."
>
> **Swap test:** marketplace apps can't claim "same walker"; local pack walkers can't claim "4 dogs". Passed.
>
> **Confidence:** segment = Medium (competitor reviews + Reddit); "nervous dogs" value = Low, only 3 own reviews mention it. **Validation:** 3 Meta ad angles at £10/day for 14 days; decision rule: the angle with the lowest cost per enquiry leads a homepage headline test if it beats the others by 30%+ after at least 20 enquiries per angle; read as directional (low volume).

**Why it's good:** starts from real alternatives with evidence, chains attributes to value in customer words, names a trigger and disqualifiers, justifies the frame, passes the swap test and labels confidence with a concrete validation plan.

### Bad

> Hearth & Hound is Manchester's leading premium pet care provider. Our passionate, experienced team delivers a world-class, seamless experience for every furry family member. Target audience: dog lovers aged 25–55. Headline: "Because your dog deserves the best."

**Why it's bad:** parity and superlative claims, unsourced "leading", a demographic instead of a segment, no alternative, no evidence, and a headline any competitor could publish unchanged.
