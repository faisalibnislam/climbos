## Reference: script shapes

Fill every slot from the lead's own words or the confirmed facts. Delete a line rather than fill it with a guess.

### First email reply to an enquiry

> Hi {name},
> {Direct answer to their question, from the facts.} {One detail they gave, echoed back.}
> To firm up the price I just need {one or two deciding details}.
> {Concrete next step with two options: "I can come and measure Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2. Which suits?"}
> {Owner first name}, {business} · {phone}

### First text (only with SMS consent or if they texted first)

> Hi {name}, it's {owner} from {business}. Thanks for asking about {job}. {One question or two time options.} Reply STOP to opt out.

### Call opener

> "Hi {name}, it's {owner} from {business}. You asked about {job} on our website {this morning / yesterday}. Is now still OK for two minutes?"
> Discovery: "What's prompting it now?" · "When would you ideally want it done?" · "Who else is involved in deciding?"
> Close: "So it's {recap}. Shall I {next step} on {option A} or {option B}?"

### Voicemail (under 20 seconds)

> "Hi {name}, {owner} from {business} about your {job} enquiry. {One useful detail.} I'll send this by email too. My number is {number}, that's {number}."

### Quote close-out

> Hi {name}, I don't want to crowd your inbox. Shall I keep the {job} quote open, or close the file for now? Either is completely fine; a one-word reply is plenty.

### Objection answer shape

> {Acknowledge in their words.} {One clarifying question, if needed.} {One proof item from the facts.} {Smaller or reversible next step.}
