Fictional business: **Tallyroo**, invoicing software for UK cleaning companies with 3–30 staff, £29/month flat. Competitors, URLs, prices and counts below are invented for illustration.

### Good (excerpt)

> **Ring 2 substitute:** "Word template plus a bank spreadsheet". Evidence: 9 of 64 competitor reviews say "switched from" a spreadsheet or Word; r/CleaningBusiness thread "how do you invoice 40 regulars?" ([url], 31 replies).
>
> **Pricing (captured 17 Oct 2026):** SparkInvoice £12 per user/month ([url]); a 10-cleaner team pays £120/month vs Tallyroo £29 flat. BrightLedger: "Contact us" (not published; signal: sales-led).
>
> **Gap (offer):** None of the 4 direct competitors bills recurring cleans automatically from a rota; 7 of 64 competitor reviews ask for it ("I still copy every Friday's jobs by hand", [url]). Why empty? Generic tools serve many trades; rota billing only matters to recurring-visit businesses. Risk: SparkInvoice could ship it; watch its changelog. Test: a homepage headline test on "Bills your regulars from the rota".
>
> **Battlecard, SparkInvoice:** Wins with sole traders who want a free tier. Claim: "Invoicing in 30 seconds" ([url]). Better than us at: mobile app ratings (4.7 vs our 4.2, [url]). Weak spots: per-user price grows with team (5 of 22 reviews). Landmine question: "What will I pay when I add my 8th cleaner?"

**Why it's good:** verbatim and dated, prices normalised with the maths, a non-product substitute with switching evidence, a gap with a why-empty check and test, and a battlecard that concedes where the rival is better.

### Bad

> Competitors: the three biggest accounting suites. They have outdated websites and weak SEO. SparkInvoice probably spends around £20k/month on ads. BrightLedger's pricing is likely £50+. Opportunity: be more modern and user-friendly, create better content, and dominate social media. Their customers hate their support.

**Why it's bad:** giants the buyer doesn't compare, no substitutes, invented ad spend and prices, hollow verdicts without observations, no counts behind "hate their support", and gaps no one can test.
