Fictional business: **Northpoint Studio**, a 2-person Shopify conversion agency in Manchester. Confirmed facts: has audited 140 Shopify product pages since 2024; runs the same 12 checks on every product page; can deliver 5 personalised video audits a week, each within 2 working days; paid offer is a product-page rebuild from £2,400; founder Alex Mensah records every audit. VOC from sales calls: "I don't know why people add to cart and then leave", "is my product page the problem or my ads?"

### Good

**Product Page Check: 12 checks on one Shopify product page, scored in your browser, plus a 5-minute video teardown from Alex within 2 working days.**

- Job: answers "is my product page the problem or my ads?" (sales-call VOC).
- Flow: paste a product URL → 7 of the 12 checks are scored automatically on screen (load, image count, price visibility, delivery info near the button and similar) → email to receive the full 12-check report and the video. 5 video slots a week; when full, the page says the next available week.
- Bridge: every failed check in the report links to "what the rebuild fixes", and the video ends with the rebuild offer from £2,400.
- Form: email + store URL. Separate unticked box: "Also send me Northpoint's emails on product-page fixes, about one a week. Unsubscribe any time." Consent stored with timestamp, IP hash, form version, text shown and tag `magnet:product-page-check`.
- Email 1 (immediate): the full report, when the video will arrive, and one question: "Which product makes you the most money?"
- Measure: URL submitted → email given → video delivered → rebuild call → rebuild sold.

### Bad

**FREE eBook: The Ultimate Guide to Ecommerce Success in 2026 (87 pages). Based on data from 10,000+ stores! Name, phone, company size and budget required. ☑ I agree to receive marketing emails (pre-ticked).**

**Why:** the good magnet answers the buyer's real pre-purchase question with Northpoint's own checks, shows value before the gate, respects the capacity of 5 a week and records consent properly; the bad one is generic content an AI can write, invents "10,000+ stores", asks for four fields and pre-ticks consent.
