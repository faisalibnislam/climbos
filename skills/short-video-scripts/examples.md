Fictional business: **Batchwise**, a meal-planning app (iOS and Android). Confirmed facts: you pick dinners for the week; the app builds one shopping list grouped by supermarket aisle and a "cook plan" that puts every step from all the recipes in one order so you cook in one session. The app's "Five easy dinners" starter plan has 5 dinners, 23 shopping-list items and a cook-plan estimate of 1 hour 35 minutes. Free plan: up to 3 dinners a week; Plus is £3.99 a month. Founder Aisha Rahman runs a faceless account: her hands, her kitchen and real screen recordings of the app can be filmed. VoC (App Store reviews): "the what's-for-dinner panic at 6pm". Search phrase: "meal prep for the week". Task: organic TikTok, reusable as a Reel.

> **Sound:** Aisha's own voiceover, recorded close to the phone in a quiet room; optional quiet track from the commercial music library only. Must work with no music.
> **Captions:** burned-in for every spoken word, centred in the middle third, clear of the bottom ~20% and right edge.
> **Caption:** Meal prep for the week: 5 dinners, 1 shopping list, 1 Sunday cook. Plan from the Batchwise app. #MealPrep #MealPrepForTheWeek #BatchCooking #MealPlanning

**Why it's good:** payoff-first opening with the search phrase spoken, on screen and in the caption; the setup uses the customer's own "6pm" phrase; every beat shows something real (actual screen recordings, real kitchen), one fact per beat, every number from the app itself; filmable in one session with no face on camera; sound-off ready; one CTA with a true offer; the end frame loops to the start.
