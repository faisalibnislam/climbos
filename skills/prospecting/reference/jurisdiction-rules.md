## Reference: jurisdiction rules for B2B prospecting

Country comes from the company's trading or registered address first, then the country-code TLD. If the two disagree, use the stricter rule. This table is a default for the agent, not legal advice; the platform's jurisdiction rules table wins where it differs.

| Country or group | May the company go on a cold list? | Conditions you must record | Opt-out deadline the sender must meet |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | Yes | Accurate sender identity, honest subject, postal address and working opt-out in every message (the writer handles this) | 10 business days (honour immediately in practice) |
| United Kingdom: companies, LLPs, Scottish partnerships, public bodies | Yes (corporate subscribers) | Legal form with its source; UK GDPR still applies to the named person, so an Article 14 source line goes in the first email | Immediately |
| United Kingdom: sole traders and non-Scottish partnerships | **No**, unless consent or soft opt-in is recorded | Legal form evidence; if unknown, hold | n/a |
| Germany, Austria | **No** (blocked by default) | n/a | n/a |
| France | Yes, if the offer relates to the recipient's professional role | Role relevance; Article 14 line | Immediately |
| Other EU/EEA countries | Only where the platform rules table allows | If no rule exists for the country, hold | Immediately |
| Canada | Only for addresses **published verbatim** by the business or person | Source URL, capture date, no "no unsolicited messages" statement on the page, role relevance. Pattern-guessed addresses blocked | 10 business days |
| Australia | Only for addresses **published verbatim** | Same evidence as Canada. Harvested lists are prohibited outright | 5 business days |

### Address-type rules

| Address type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Personal work address published on the company site | Preferred. Record URL and date. |
| Pattern-inferred work address | At most 3 candidates per person, verified before use; blocked for Canada and Australia; record "inferred". |
| Role address (info, hello, contact, sales, support, admin, office, team, enquiries, inquiries, billing, accounts, hr, jobs, careers, press, marketing) | Only for companies with 1–10 employees and only when no personal work address is published. 10% of the list or less. Flag "no first name". |
| noreply, postmaster, abuse, webmaster | Never. |
| Disposable domain | Never. |
| Free webmail | Only when a sole trader outside the UK and EU publishes it as their business contact. |

### Data you may and may not hold

- **May:** company name, domain, address, industry, size band, legal form, public services and prices, dated public triggers, a named person's name, work title and work email from a public business source.
- **May not:** data scraped from LinkedIn, restricted-visibility profiles, personal phone numbers, home addresses, personal social accounts, or anything about health, family, religion, politics or private life.
