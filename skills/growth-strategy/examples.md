Fictional business: **Mossgate Mobile Bikes**, a one-van mobile bicycle repair service in Bristol. Confirmed facts: average job £85, owner estimates 55% gross margin, about 30 jobs a month, budget £400/month, owner has 5 hours a week for marketing, 61 Google reviews at 4.9. Numbers are invented for illustration.

### Good (excerpt)

> **What works now:** owner's booking notes for the last 40 jobs: 17 Google search/Maps, 12 repeat, 8 word of mouth, 3 other. Protect GBP and repeat first.
>
> **Diagnosis:** demand capture, not awareness. "Mobile bike repair Bristol" shows the map pack; Mossgate appears for its own name only (checked 17 Oct 2026). Falsifier: if GBP views don't rise within 3 weeks of the fixes, the constraint is elsewhere.
>
> **Affordable CAC:** £85 × 55% = £46.75 gross profit on the first job. Owner caps acquisition at 50% of that: about £23 per new customer (assumption: owner's cap; confirm week 1).
>
> **Bullseye:** Primary: Google Business Profile plus Google Search ads on 6 high-intent terms, £300/month, with offline import of booked jobs. Secondary: partnerships with 3 office buildings that have bike storage (monthly on-site service day). Hygiene: review ask by SMS after every job (opted-in customers only). Not doing: Instagram (no time; social posting isn't how people find a repairer), blog SEO (informational clicks shrinking under AI answers), leaflets (no tracking at this budget).
>
> **North Star:** 45 jobs/month by 15 Jan, from 30. Leading indicators: GBP calls + bookings 12/week (baseline 7), cost per booked job ≤ £23, new reviews 3/week.
>
> **Resources:** ads £300 + call tracking £20 = £320 of £400; hours 1.5 GBP + 1 ads review + 2 partnerships = 4.5 of 5.
>
> **Week 1:** (1) Add "how did you hear about us?" to the booking form. (2) Complete GBP services, areas and 10 job photos. (3) Build one Search campaign, 6 exact/phrase terms, negatives list. (4) Email 3 building managers. (5) Set up the weekly scorecard sheet.

**Why it's good:** protects what already works, names one constraint with a falsifier, computes CAC with the assumption labelled, commits to one primary and one secondary with reasons for every rejection, and the money and hours add up.

### Bad

> To grow, Mossgate should build an omnichannel presence: post daily on Instagram and TikTok, start a blog, run Facebook and Google ads, launch a YouTube channel, and partner with influencers. Industry data shows 78% of cyclists search online for repairs. Aim to double revenue. Consider a loyalty programme and referral scheme too.

**Why it's bad:** a channel buffet that ignores 5 hours a week and £400, an invented statistic, no diagnosis, no CAC check, no not-doing list, no leading indicators, and nothing the owner can start tomorrow.
