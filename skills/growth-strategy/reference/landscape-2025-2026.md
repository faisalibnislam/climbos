## Reference: 2025–2026 landscape adjustments

Most of these figures come from vendors or single studies. Quote them with their source name and as ranges; never round them into certainty.

| Shift | What the evidence says | Adjustment to make in the plan |
|---|---|---|
| **AI search shrinks informational clicks** | Pew (Jul 2025): users clicked a traditional result in 8% of Google visits with an AI summary vs. 15% without. Seer Interactive: organic CTR on AI Overview queries fell 61% (Jun 2024–Sep 2025); pages cited in the overview got about 35% more clicks than uncited ones. Ahrefs: position-one CTR down 58% on AI Overview keywords (Dec 2025). Google disputes these findings. AI-referred visitors can convert well (Ahrefs' own site: about 0.5% of visits, 12.1% of sign-ups; single-site data). | Don't make informational blog SEO a primary engine without strong justification. Prefer high-intent pages: comparison, alternatives, pricing, "best X for Y", local service pages, original data and tools. Add **AI answer visibility**: consistent business facts, third-party reviews, genuine forum presence. Track AI referrals (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini) as a separate source; run a monthly prompt check ("best [category] for [segment] in [city]") and record whether and how the business is described. |
| **Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia are heavily cited by AI engines** | Cross-engine citation studies (Peec, LLM Pulse, 2026). | Genuine participation where buyers ask questions; no astroturfing. |
| **Local search** | Studies disagree widely on AI Overview frequency for local queries (about 8% to 88% by query mix). The map pack still dominates "near me" queries. | Google Business Profile completeness and review velocity remain the highest-leverage local asset. |
| **Agentic browsing** | Chrome auto-browse (May 2026) can fail on unlabelled forms and heavy cookie walls. | Labelled forms and light consent flows are now a conversion issue. |
| **Meta Andromeda** | Meta's retrieval engine (announced Dec 2024) matches ads to people with a much larger model; practitioners report detailed targeting behaves like a suggestion and creative diversity does the targeting. Detailed-targeting exclusions were removed in 2025; custom-audience exclusions remain. | Fewer campaigns and ad sets, broad audiences, 3–5 genuinely different creative concepts (angle, persona, format) drawn from the VoC bank. Positioning research becomes creative research. |
| **Google automation** | AI Max for Search adds broad-match and keywordless expansion, text customisation and final-URL expansion. Local Services Ads migrate into Google Ads as Performance Max pay-per-lead campaigns from Aug 2026 through 2027. A Feb 2026 vendor study of 888 contractors put average LSA cost per lead around $53. | Negative keywords, search-term review and conversion quality (offline conversion import for booked jobs) matter more, because automation optimises toward whatever signal it is fed. |
| **LinkedIn organic reach** | Creator reports estimate organic reach fell roughly 40–50% year on year in 2025; company pages reach a small single-digit share of followers. Person-led content and Thought Leader Ads outperform brand-page posts in practitioner reports. | B2B plans centre on the founder's or employees' profiles, not the company page. LinkedIn ads below roughly $1.5–3k/month rarely gather enough data. |
| **ChatGPT ads** | Launched Feb 2026 for large advertisers; self-serve for smaller US businesses arrived around mid-2026 per trade reports. | At most a small experimental line until attribution matures. |
| **Measurement** | Chrome kept third-party cookies (Apr 2025) and Google retired most Privacy Sandbox APIs. Safari 26 turned on Advanced Fingerprinting Protection by default; click IDs (gclid, fbclid) are stripped in Mail, Messages and Private Browsing, while UTMs are kept. About 20 US states have privacy laws (Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island from 1 Jan 2026), generally with opt-outs for targeted advertising. | UTMs on every link; server-side conversions (Meta Conversions API, Google Enhanced Conversions) where the stack allows; a "how did you hear about us?" question; a weekly scorecard that doesn't depend on platform attribution alone. |
