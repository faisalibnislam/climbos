## Reference: channel options, traps and indicators by business type

| Type | Usual primary engines | Typical traps | Key unit-economics question | Leading indicators (weekly) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2B SaaS (SMB, under $100/mo)** | Product-led (free trial or freemium plus onboarding), integration marketplaces, comparison and alternatives pages, founder-led content, review sites | Outbound and LinkedIn ads at a price that can't carry them; "content" with no distribution plan | Payback on blended CAC; trial→paid rate | Qualified sign-ups, activation rate (reached first value), trial→paid, reply rate on any outbound |
| **B2B SaaS (mid-ticket, $5k+ ACV)** | Founder-led sales plus outbound to a tight list, LinkedIn (founder posts, Thought Leader Ads), partnerships, webinars, review sites | Expecting pure product-led growth; vanity followers | Pipeline per week; win rate vs. alternatives | Conversations started, demos booked, pipeline created, win rate |
| **B2C app** | Referral and virality loops, app-store optimisation, short-form creator content, paid social once retention is proven | Paying to acquire users who churn in week 1 | D1/D7/D30 retention before any spend | Install→signup, D1/D7 retention, invites sent per user, cost per retained user |
| **Local service** | Google Business Profile plus reviews, Local Services Ads / search ads, referrals and repeat, partnerships (realtors, property managers), Nextdoor | Social posting as "marketing"; generic national SEO content | Cost per booked job; review velocity | Calls and form fills, booked-job rate, GBP actions, new reviews, quote→win rate |
| **Ecommerce / DTC** | Meta / TikTok paid with heavy creative testing, creators, email/SMS retention, marketplaces (Amazon, TikTok Shop) | Scaling ads before contribution margin is positive; discount addiction | Contribution margin per order; repeat rate at 90 days | CTR and thumb-stop per creative, add-to-cart rate, contribution margin per order, email capture rate, 60/90-day repeat |
| **Agency / consultant** | Referrals and network, niche specialisation, founder LinkedIn, case studies, outbound to a tight list, partnerships | Being a generalist; pitching services instead of outcomes | Pipeline from 1–2 niches; close rate; retainer length | Conversations started, discovery calls booked, proposal→close rate, referral asks made |
| **Creator / coach** | One content platform done well plus an email list, collaborations, a low-ticket offer feeding a high-ticket one | Spreading across 5 platforms; no owned list | Email subscribers per week; offer conversion | Email subs, saves and shares, DM enquiries, low-ticket conversion |

**Good vs. mediocre channel choice (DTC candle brand, $1,500/month)**
- Mediocre: "Leverage Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, SEO, email, influencers, Google Shopping and Meta ads for an omnichannel presence."
- Expert: "Primary: Meta ads, because AOV is $48 with 65% margin, giving about $30 affordable CAC on the first order; 6–10 creative concepts at $40/day. Secondary: 20 micro-creator gifting seeds a month, reused as ad creative. Hygiene: post-purchase email flow. Not now: SEO (12+ month payback, AI Overviews eating informational clicks), Pinterest (no in-house design time), TikTok Shop (fulfilment SLA risk)."
