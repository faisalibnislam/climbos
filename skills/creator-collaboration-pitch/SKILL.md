## Purpose

Write a respectful collaboration invitation that gives the creator a concrete reason to consider the idea. A creator is a potential partner, not a media slot with a guaranteed audience. The pitch must make sense for both the business and the creator, use a public content example as a relevant reference, and leave payment, product access, timing, and deliverables open for owner confirmation.

## Inputs

- The business's confirmed offer, audience, and any owner-approved collaboration budget or terms.
- The creator's supported audience fit and relevant need.
- Public content examples with exact URLs and observations. These are research notes, not proof the sender watched or used the content.
- Any prior competitor-collaboration evidence, with its quote, publication date when shown, and observation timestamp. A past collaboration is not proof of current exclusivity.

If essential offer terms or audience need are unknown, keep the pitch exploratory and ask the owner to confirm the missing detail. Never invent a rate, audience demographic, result, product experience, exclusivity, or deadline.

## Procedure

1. Select one exact content sample from this creator. Use its source URL as `evidenceUrl`; never borrow another creator's example.
2. State the relevant public topic plainly and connect it to the creator's supported audience or content theme. Do not flatter or imply personal familiarity.
3. Offer one collaboration format as a proposal. Mark paid, gifted, affiliate, or co-created terms for owner confirmation unless the owner supplied current terms.
4. Explain the likely value for the creator and audience without promising reach, engagement, revenue, or conversions.
5. Ask one low-pressure question. Keep the first message concise and make it easy to decline.
6. Include only approved follow-ups that add useful information. Stop after a reply, decline, or opt-out.
7. Leave the draft for owner review. The draft is not permission to send or publish.

## Output rules

- Every personalized opener must include the exact `evidenceUrl` for a content sample belonging to that creator. Code rejects an unmatched URL.
- Do not say “I watched,” “I loved,” “we use,” or similar unless the owner supplied that fact.
- Do not treat visible views, likes, comments, or followers as likely campaign reach or proof of results.
- Do not describe an older competitor collaboration as current or exclusive without dated evidence that establishes that state.
- Do not state unconfirmed fees, deliverables, product access, or schedule as agreed.
- The creator and business must both be able to review and approve the final terms before any send.
