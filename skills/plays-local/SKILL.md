## Rules every local play inherits

- **FTC review rule (16 CFR 465, in force Oct 2024):** no fake or AI-written reviews, no sentiment-conditioned incentives, no undisclosed insider reviews, no company-run "independent" review sites, no suppressing reviews by threats, no bought followers/views. ~$51,744 per violation (2024).
- **Google review policy is stricter:** no incentive of any kind, even neutral; no gating (asking only happy customers); no discouraging negatives; no requiring or pressuring reviews on the premises; no staff quotas. Yelp forbids soliciting reviews at all.
- **Fair review invitation:** for each campaign, use one eligible public destination and send every eligible recipient the same neutral invitation, regardless of satisfaction, private feedback, sentiment, or predicted rating. Keep private feedback optional and equally visible; never reward, fabricate, or post a review for a customer.
- **TCPA/SMS:** marketing texts need prior express written consent; FCC one-to-one rule vacated (Jan 2025) but carriers may still require it. Quiet hours 8am–9pm local; Florida FTSA 8am–8pm, max 3/24h, $500–$1,500 per message; other state mini-TCPAs. Honor STOP; register 10DLC.
- **CAN-SPAM:** accurate headers/subjects, postal address, opt-out honored within 10 business days; ~$53,088 per email.
- **Endorsements:** any comp, free meal or fee is disclosed clearly in the post itself (#hosted/#comped are unclear); never ask a creator to hide it; referrers posting codes disclose rewards.
- **Healthcare:** HIPAA limits patient data in marketing and review replies (never confirm someone is a patient); state boards restrict testimonials and referral inducements; anti-kickback with federal payers; counsel reviews any referral incentive.

## How to use this catalog

- **Fix the leaky bucket first.** Plays 1–8 lift the conversion of everything else. Check them before suggesting acquisition plays 12+: a 3.9 rating, 40% missed calls and no rebooking waste money on mail, creators or sponsorships.
- **Suggest first, by vertical:** Home services 2, 1, 5, 8, 12, 15, 25, 18 · Food & beverage 1, 11, 13, 14, 24, 26, 29, 20 · Fitness 9, 3, 17, 16, 10, 1, 28 · Salons/spas 4, 1, 3, 10, 16, 33, 30 · Clinics 4, 3, 2, 1, 16, 17, 18 · Local retail 11, 20, 30, 37, 22, 1 · Professional services 2, 1, 15, 38, 28, 8 (Screened), 22.
- **Best effort-to-upside creative plays** (a "find the right 50 targets and pitch them" step owners rarely do and AI can): 13, 14, 15, 23, 25, 26, 27, 31, 33.
- **Tracking needed for nearly every play:** unique codes per partner/route/creator, dedicated call-tracking numbers, QR-coded landing pages, a "how did you hear about us" field, and a cost-per-booked-job ledger across channels. Without them plays 12–40 can't be judged.
- **Scores:** predictability/leverage map the source's H/M/L to 4/3/2. Evidence strong = regulator, platform policy or peer-reviewed study; moderate = large vendor dataset with stated sample; weak = practitioner/vendor claims or anecdotes (source tag [WEAK EVIDENCE]).
- **Caveats:** most local stats come from vendors selling the channel; treat ranges as planning priors and replace them with the owner's data after 4–8 weeks. [practitioner estimate] = no published source. "secondary" = cited via a write-up, not the primary report.

## Plays

### 1. Compliant review-velocity engine (`review-velocity-engine`) · evidence strong · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** any business with ~20+ jobs/visits a month, a GBP and customer phones/emails.
- **Why:** reviews can influence local discovery and buying decisions; available survey figures report that some customers who are open to reviewing do not leave one without an invitation. Use a neutral invitation rather than targeting people by satisfaction.
- **Do:** 1) GBP short link + QR on invoices, receipts, counter; 2) send every eligible customer the same neutral invitation and public destination, with no sentiment pre-screen; SMS/email only with appropriate permission; 3) one reminder only where the chosen platform and contact rules allow it; 4) ask without pressure and never wait while the customer writes; 5) choose a different eligible platform only for a separate evidence-supported campaign; Yelp signage only; 6) embed all reviews on site.
- **Expect:** 74% want reviews from the last 3 months; 4.5+-only buyers rose 17%→31%; 96% open to reviewing, 29% did [brightlocal.com]. Email asks 8–15% response [fera.ai]; SMS + verbal 15–30% [practitioner estimate]. +1 Yelp star ≈ +5–9% revenue, independents only [harvardmagazine.com].
- **Cost / time:** $0–$60/mo; 2–3h + 15 min/wk; first reviews 1–7 days, rating shift 4–8 wks.
- **Track:** request-to-review %; days since last review (<7); new-review stars.
- **Rules & risks:** same invitation and destination for every eligible recipient; no sentiment filtering, incentives, gating, quotas, fake/AI/staff reviews, or customer review posting; clinics: don't prompt treatment details.
- **ClimbOS does:** link/QR, auto-asks from job/POS feed, dashboard, no-review alert. Owner: verbal ask, POS hookup.

### 2. Speed-to-lead + missed-call text-back (`speed-to-lead-missed-call`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** home services, clinics, salons, law/accounting, gyms; a VoIP line that can text.
- **Why:** leads decay in minutes and unanswered callers ring the next map-pack result.
- **Do:** 1) baseline a week of answer rate and callback time; 2) missed-call auto-text with question + booking link; 3) all forms, LSA, marketplace leads into one inbox; 4) human callback <5 min in hours, next morning after; 5) weekly response-time report.
- **Expect:** home services missed 14% of calls (CallRail 2025) vs 52% answered live (Invoca 2026); 78% abandoned a business after an unanswered call [getaira.io, usecarly.com; secondary]. 5 vs 30 min response ≈ 21x qualify odds (2007 vendor study) [retellai.com]. Recover 20–40% of missed calls [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $20–$100/mo; 2–4h; same day.
- **Track:** % answered live; median response minutes; jobs from recovered calls.
- **Rules & risks:** reply to inbound callers is fine, promo lists need written consent; missed calls hurt LSA ranking [support.google.com].
- **ClimbOS does:** text-back, unified inbox, AI first replies, "leads you lost" report. Owner: callbacks.

### 3. Dormant-customer reactivation sprint (`dormant-reactivation`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** 12+ months of history: dental/vet recall, salons, lapsed gym members, HVAC, accountants.
- **Why:** lapsed customers already trust you; inertia, not dissatisfaction, is the usual cause.
- **Do:** 1) bucket by last visit 6–12, 12–18, 18–24, 24+ mo; 2) check consent per channel; 3) one reason to return per bucket; 4) 3–5 touches in 14 days: email, text, staff call, email; 5) link with two specific slots; repeat quarterly.
- **Expect:** dental 25–35% (6–12 mo), 15–25% (12–18), 10–15% (18–24), <8% beyond; one touch 3–5% vs 15–25% multi-touch [ainora.lt, clerri.com; vendor]. Other verticals 5–15% of a 6–18-mo list [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–$200; 4–6h + 1–2h/wk calls; 2–7 days.
- **Track:** booked rate per touch; contact rate; reactivated revenue.
- **Rules & risks:** SMS consent; CAN-SPAM; no PHI in subject lines; skip opt-outs.
- **ClimbOS does:** bucketing, sequences, sends, call lists, revenue tracking. Owner: calls, offers.

### 4. Rebook-before-they-leave (`rebook-before-leave`) · evidence weak · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** dental, physio, chiro, vet, salons, barbers, spas, recurring lawn/pool/cleaning, PT.
- **Why:** the cheapest booking is made while the customer is still in the chair.
- **Do:** 1) interval per service (6-mo hygiene, 6-wk color, 4-wk cut); 2) checkout offers two next slots; 3) weekly prebook board per staff (coach, no penalties); 4) reminders at 7d and 1d; 5) decliners get a "pick your time" link 2 wks before due.
- **Expect:** prebooked dental patients return 80–90% vs 35–45%; avg recall 60–70%, best 85–95% [getviva.ai, ainora.lt; vendor]. Salons +10–20 pts prebook in 60 days [practitioner estimate]. Mechanism robust, magnitudes weak.
- **Cost / time:** $0; 1h training; immediate.
- **Track:** prebook % by staff; no-show %; days between visits.
- **Rules & risks:** reminders are transactional, no promos without consent; HIPAA-minimal.
- **ClimbOS does:** reminders, rebook links, prebook dashboard. Owner: coaching.

### 5. Maintenance membership / service plan (`maintenance-membership`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** HVAC, plumbing, pest, lawn, pool, cleaning, detailing, vet/dental plans, car washes; spare shoulder-season capacity.
- **Why:** recurring revenue that fills slow seasons and puts techs where repairs are found.
- **Do:** 1) one plan: 2 visits/yr, priority, waived diagnostic, 10–15% off repairs; 2) bill monthly ($19–$29) or yearly; 3) techs offer it after every paid call; 4) on invoice, site, post-job text; 5) schedule visits in shoulder seasons, renewal touch 30 days out.
- **Expect:** 35–55% take it after a paid diagnostic vs 1–4% cold [plyrium.com; vendor]. $150–$500/yr [pipelineon.com]. Margin 55–65% vs 35–45% repairs [applausehq.com]. 3-yr revenue $1,640/member vs $480 [plyrium.com]. Renewal ~86% [webtonic.io; secondary].
- **Cost / time:** $0–$100/mo; 6–10h; sign-ups 1–4 wks, flywheel 6–12 mo.
- **Track:** offers per tech/wk; attach rate; churn.
- **Rules & risks:** ROSCA/state auto-renewal laws; no upsell pressure; dental plans aren't insurance.
- **ClimbOS does:** pricing calculator, billing page, tech scripts, renewals. Owner: training.

### 6. Google Business Profile foundation (post-Q&A era) (`gbp-foundation`) · evidence strong · predictability 4 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** every local business; service-area businesses hide address but verify.
- **Why:** primary category, proximity and name keywords top local-pack factors (Whitespark 2026); Gemini "Ask Maps" replaced Q&A in late 2025.
- **Do:** 1) most specific primary category (test vs top 3 rivals); 2) fill every field (priced services, hours, booking); 3) 10+ real photos, then 2–4/wk; 4) site FAQ + schema for old Q&A topics; 5) weekly posts; identical NAP everywhere.
- **Expect:** photos → 42% more direction requests, 35% more site clicks (Google, via agencies) [mediagistic.com]. Weekly posters 42% more direction requests (correlational) [wisernotify.com; secondary]. Ignore "520% more calls" claims (no source).
- **Cost / time:** $0; 3–5h + 30 min/wk; 2–8 wks.
- **Track:** calls; direction requests; site clicks.
- **Rules & risks:** keyword-stuffed names, fake addresses → suspension.
- **ClimbOS does:** audit, category recs, posts, photo uploads, FAQ schema, NAP checks. Owner: verification, photos.

### 7. Reply to every review, non-templated (`review-reply-system`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** any business with reviews on Google, Yelp, Facebook or industry sites.
- **Why:** future customers read replies as proof the owner fixes problems.
- **Do:** 1) reply within 48h; 2) positive: name, their detail, one natural service line; 3) negative: acknowledge, take offline, follow up publicly; 4) report fakes via GBP, never threaten; 5) monthly themes → fix causes.
- **Expect:** 80% favor businesses replying to all reviews; 50% put off by templates (BrightLocal 2026) [pinmeto.com]. 89% expect replies [brightlocal.com]. No isolated revenue lift.
- **Cost / time:** $0; 10–20 min/wk; 1–3 mo.
- **Track:** reply rate; reply time; negatives resolved.
- **Rules & risks:** FTC suppression rule; no refunds for deletions; HIPAA.
- **ClimbOS does:** monitoring, unique drafts for one-tap approval, alerts. Owner: approvals.

### 9. Paid multi-visit intro offer (`paid-intro-offer`) · evidence weak · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** boutique fitness, yoga/pilates, martial arts, climbing, massage, med spa, physio, salons.
- **Why:** paying filters freebie seekers, builds the habit and sets up a conversion talk.
- **Do:** 1) replace "first class free" with 2–4 wks or 3–5 visits at ~30–50% of monthly; 2) book visit 2 at visit 1; 3) named coach, mid-point check-in, consult 3 days before expiry; 4) credit intro price if they join early; 5) chase non-converters at 14 and 45 days.
- **Expect:** paid intros ~50–60% convert vs free trials often <20% [vibefam.com]. >3-visit intros earn most; ~2% of booking-platform visitors become members [athletechnews.com; secondary].
- **Cost / time:** $0–$500; 4h; 2–4 wks.
- **Track:** intro sales/wk; % booking visit 2 in 7 days; consult show rate.
- **Rules & risks:** auto-conversion needs disclosure and consent (ROSCA, health-club laws).
- **ClimbOS does:** offer page, nurture, follow-ups, funnel dashboard. Owner: consults.

### 10. Double-sided referral program with tracking (`referral-program`) · evidence strong · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** all; best for high-ticket or high-trust services.
- **Why:** referred customers arrive pre-trusted and churn less.
- **Do:** 1) two-sided credit (e.g., $50/$50) or charity donation; 2) personal code at the moment of delight; 3) specific ask ("who on your street has an older furnace?"); 4) thank within 24h, handwritten for big jobs; 5) monthly share, conversion, cost.
- **Expect:** referred customers' lifetime value 16–25% higher (bank study) [wharton.upenn.edu]. 2–8% of customers refer yearly [practitioner estimate]. HVAC rewards $50–$75 service, $100–$150 installs [christopholivierconsulting.com].
- **Cost / time:** ~5–15% of first-job value [practitioner estimate]; 3–4h; 2–6 wks.
- **Track:** codes shared; referral bookings; referrer repeat rate.
- **Rules & risks:** no paid patient referrals; RESPA; code posters disclose; never reward reviews.
- **ClimbOS does:** codes, cards, asks, attribution, thank-you drafts. Owner: reward, notes.

### 11. Pre-stamped loyalty card (endowed progress) (`endowed-progress-loyalty`) · evidence strong · predictability 4 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** repeat purchases: cafes, bakeries, car washes, barbers, juice bars, groomers, QSR.
- **Why:** people persist toward goals they feel already started.
- **Do:** 1) "buy 12, get 1" with 2–3 pre-applied stamps instead of a blank 10; 2) give a reason (welcome, birthday); 3) digital card captures consented contacts; 4) "you're 2 away" nudge; 5) compare vs old card for 90 days.
- **Expect:** car-wash field test: 34% completion with 2 free stamps on a 10-stamp card vs 19% on a blank 8-stamp card (~300 cards, replicated in labs) [knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu].
- **Cost / time:** $0–$50/mo; 1–2h; 2–6 wks.
- **Track:** completion rate; stamps at 30 days; visit frequency.
- **Rules & risks:** SMS needs consent; clear expiry terms; no stamps for reviews.
- **ClimbOS does:** card design, nudges, cohort analysis. Owner: POS, staff habits.

### 22. AI-assistant and Apple/Bing visibility (`ai-answer-visibility`) · evidence weak · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** all; most for considered buys (dentist, lawyer, contractor).
