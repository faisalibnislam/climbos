## How to use this catalog

- **Scores.** Ordered by predictability × leverage. 5 high (leverage "very high" for featuring and retail open call), 4 medium-high, 3 medium, 2 low-medium, 1 low. Mixed source grades (weak-moderate; moderate process, weak outcomes) rounded down to `weak`.
- **Run order.** Reviews first (Vine on Amazon): traffic to 0-review pages is wasted. Affiliate before seeding, gift guides, YouTube. DTC loop: seeding/TikTok Shop → license winners → creator-handle ads. Faire builds proof for retail open calls; the impact angle multiplies both.
- **DTC/makers:** reviews, affiliate, seeding (TikTok Shop if AOV < $60), referral, Merchant Center; gift guides and Faire seasonally; subscribe-and-save if consumable; Vine and Brand Referral Bonus on Amazon.
- **Apps:** page tests and review-prompt timing lift every install source; recap cards and referral add inflow; nominate for featuring every release.
- **Creators, courses, coaching:** paid newsletter recommendations and JV partners fill a challenge or webinar; BookBub feeds the same funnel for authors.
- **Evidence.** Most benchmarks come from vendors selling the channel (ReferralCandy, Octane AI, AmpliSell, Firework, Shopify): treat as ceilings, plan on the low half, never guarantee. "[practitioner estimate]" is the researcher's synthesis.
- **Legal review before shipping as templates (marked LEGAL REVIEW):** giveaway rules, auto-renew subscription flows, sustainability claims, health claims in any creator or host brief, course earnings claims.
- **All creator, affiliate, review plays:** FTC Endorsement Guides: free product must be disclosed; brands liable for endorsers' claims. FTC 2024 fake-review rule: no sentiment-conditioned incentives, suppression, undisclosed insider reviews or bought followers. Re-check at runtime: ChatGPT checkout, Vine, Faire, TikTok Shop fees; click-to-cancel status.
- **Not covered (gaps):** paid sampling networks, subscription boxes, Amazon Creator Connections, journalist-request platforms (Qwoted, Featured), Etsy Ads fees.

## Plays

### Opt-in creator seeding funnel (`creator-seeding-optin-funnel`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** DTC or makers with a photogenic product, landed COGS < ~$25, GM ≥55%, ≥50 units/mo to gift.
- **Why:** Asking "want one?" before shipping removes the waste of untargeted gifting (94% send product, 19% see real advocacy).
- **Do:** 1) 3 audience personas, skip creators with >3 sponsored of last 10 posts; 2) list 300–500/mo (free LTK Brand Platform helps); 3) opt-in pitch citing one post, no obligation; 4) ship in 48h with code and approved claims; 5) follow up day 7 and 21, ask rights, invite to affiliate; 6) kill worst persona monthly.
- **Expect:** response 3–35% [elev8or.io]; vendor: ~30% respond, 80% ship, 30–50% of shipped post [getsaral.com]; 300 pitches → 45–90 opt-ins → 15–40 posts, 1–3 of 30 creators drive most sales [practitioner estimate]; no-opt-in post rate 19–30% [hireinfluence.com].
- **Cost / time:** $10–35/creator; 6–10 h/mo; posts in 10–21 days.
- **Track:** opt-in ≥15%; ship-to-post ≤21 days; code use within 72h.
- **Rules & risks:** #gifted/#ad even without obligation; brand liable for creator claims (LEGAL REVIEW for health claims); never condition on positive review.
- **ClimbOS does:** list, scoring, pitches, opt-in form, claims card, tracking. Owner: approve, ship, DM.

### TikTok Shop sample-to-content machine (`tiktok-shop-sample-machine`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** US/UK DTC, AOV < ~$60, demo-able, GM ≥60%, can meet shipping SLA. Not high-consideration or regulated.
- **Why:** Creators request free samples, each yields several shoppable videos, and sample→video→GMV ratios are plannable.
- **Do:** 1) 1–3 hero SKUs, commission 15–20% open, 20–30% targeted; 2) approve only ≥1k followers, category GMV, ≥3 posts/wk; 3) ~30 samples/mo, ramp toward 200; 4) brief: 3 hooks, banned claims, post in 7–14 days; 5) Spark-boost top 5%; 6) re-sample producers only.
- **Expect:** at $0–12.5k GMV/mo ~30 samples → ~180 videos, 51% of GMV from creators; at ~$100k/mo ~400 samples, 73% [amplisell.com]; no verified ROI [syncly.app]; failure state: GMV < $1k/mo from over-approval [amplisell.com].
- **Cost / time:** samples + 15–30% commission + fees; ~5 h/wk; videos in 2–4 wks, stable ratio 2–3 mo.
- **Track:** sample→video in 14 days; % videos with an order; GMV per sample.
- **Rules & risks:** fees 35–45% before COGS [fivex.com]; SLA penalties; commercial toggle plus FTC disclosure; LEGAL REVIEW for health claims.
- **ClimbOS does:** shortlist, invites, briefs, hooks, non-poster flags, weekly economics. Owner: shop, approvals, shipping, boosts.

### Post-delivery review and photo-review engine (`review-photo-engine`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** DTC or makers with ≥30 orders/mo; biggest win on SKUs with < 5 reviews and pricier items.
- **Why:** Most of the effect is 0 → ~5 reviews, and asking at delivery, not shipment, collects them mechanically.
- **Do:** 1) bucket SKUs 0 / 1–4 / 5+; 2) trigger at delivered + time to first use (food 3d, skincare 10d, apparel 14d after returns); 3) one-tap stars, optional photo; 4) same incentive for any honest review; 5) one reminder at +5 days; 6) same-day reply to 1–3 stars, syndicate photos.
- **Expect:** 5–15% of orders review, so 5 reviews needs ~50–100 orders [practitioner estimate]; 5+ reviews up to 270% more likely bought (380% high-priced) [spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu], a ceiling; causal estimates ~5–9% revenue per star [cleancommit.io].
- **Cost / time:** app $0–60/mo; 3–4 h setup; 2–4 wks.
- **Track:** submit rate; photo attach rate; revenue share from 5+ review SKUs.
- **Rules & risks:** FTC 2024 bans sentiment-tied incentives and suppression; never hide negatives; Amazon bans incentives (use Vine).
- **ClimbOS does:** bucketing, request flows, replies, review mining for FAQ and hooks. Owner: app, fixes.

### Post-purchase referral with cash or commission reward (`post-purchase-referral`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** repeat or giftable DTC with ≥100 orders/mo, subscription boxes; margin for a $10–20 reward or 10–15% commission.
- **Why:** 83% say they'd refer but 29% do; prompting at peak satisfaction with cash or commission (2.7–4.5× coupons) closes the gap.
- **Do:** 1) friend 15% off, referrer $10–20 credit or cash (test vs coupon); 2) prompt on thank-you page, shipping and delivery emails, QR insert; 3) personal link plus speakable code; 4) pre-written SMS/WhatsApp/DM text; 5) monthly "you've earned X"; 6) review at 90 days.
- **Expect:** share-click 4.64% top quarter, 13.38% top 10%; first referral ~14 days median [referralcandy.com]; referred visitors convert ~3–5% [referralcandy.com]; mature 5–10% of revenue [eightx.co], plan 2–6% year one [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** app $0–200/mo + rewards; ~4 h; ~2 wks.
- **Track:** view→share-click; shares per sharer; referred visit→order.
- **Rules & risks:** cash may need tax reporting; personal channels only; public referrers must disclose.
- **ClimbOS does:** prompt and share copy, insert card, reward tests, reports. Owner: app, budget.

### UGC ad library plus creator-handle ads (`ugc-ad-library-creator-handle-ads`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** DTC and apps spending ≥$1k/mo on Meta or TikTok, or licensing seeding output.
- **Why:** Cheap UGC supplies many hooks against fatigue, and running winners from the creator's handle (Spark, Partnership Ads) adds social proof.
- **Do:** 1) brief 5 hook angles; 2) 8–12 videos from 4–6 creators, 3 hooks each; 3) 6–12 mo paid rights upfront; 4) creator-handle ads vs brand-handle control; 5) kill at ~2× target CPA; 6) 4–6 new videos/mo.
- **Expect:** TikTok self-reported Spark: +142% engagement, +43% CVR, −4% CPM; agency claims 67% lower CPA (unverified) [attnagency.com]; 1 in 5–10 videos becomes a scalable winner [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $150–200/video median, $50–100 entry [billo.app]; rights +$150–300/yr [idukki.io]; 2–3 wks + 7–14 days testing.
- **Track:** 3-second hook rate; CPA by hook; creative half-life.
- **Rules & risks:** no paid use without written licence; keep "Paid partnership" label; substantiate before/after claims (LEGAL REVIEW for health).
- **ClimbOS does:** briefs, hooks, shortlist, licence terms, rights tracking, analysis. Owner: spend, licences.

### Paid newsletter recommendations (`paid-newsletter-recommendations`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** creators, courses, coaching, consumer apps with a newsletter-first funnel and an estimable sub→customer rate.
- **Why:** Other newsletters recommend you and you pay per subscriber (beehiiv refunds unengaged), so unit cost is fixed.
- **Do:** 1) max CPS = sub→customer rate × first-year margin × 0.5; 2) beehiiv Boosts or SparkLoop offer; 3) approve matching publishers only; 4) pause publishers whose 30-day cohorts don't open; 5) welcome sequence sells within 7 days.
- **Expect:** $1.50–3.00/sub, $1.63 early average [beehiiv.com]; one creator ~$2 per quality sub [beehiiv.com]; 500 subs at $2 with 1–3% buying $49 in 60 days → $245–735 [practitioner estimate]: profitable only with a back end.
- **Cost / time:** CPS only; ~2 h; days.
- **Track:** boosted vs organic opens; 14-day unsubscribes; welcome conversion.
- **Rules & risks:** weak cohorts; explicit consent, double opt-in in EU.
- **ClimbOS does:** CPS cap, offer copy, publisher screening, cohorts, welcome flow. Owner: fund.

### Micro-newsletter sponsorship ladder (`micro-newsletter-sponsorship-ladder`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** all segments in niches with many 2k–50k-sub newsletters.
- **Why:** Niche newsletters sell under-bought flat slots with editor endorsement; cheap tests plus rebooking winners make it predictable.
- **Do:** 1) list 30–60 with ad routes (Passionfroot, beehiiv, Paved); 2) 5 tests at $50–250; 3) unique link and code each; 4) after 2 wks rebook top 2 on 3-issue deals; 5) add 5 tests/mo; 6) creators: swap if lists within 2×.
- **Expect:** consumer CPM ~$15–35 [beehiiv.com]; classifieds $50–250 under 50k [tryflowdrive.com]; CTR 0.5–2% of opens (10k list → ~20–90 clicks); 1 in 4–5 tests beats paid social [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $250–1,250 per round; 1–3 wks.
- **Track:** CTR and CPC per slot; survey share; code use.
- **Rules & risks:** sponsored label; inflated counts (ask for open-rate screenshots); surveys undercount.
- **ClimbOS does:** discovery, inquiries, ad variants, UTMs, leaderboard. Owner: book, pay.

### Gift-guide editor pipeline (`gift-guide-editor-pipeline`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** giftable products at clear price points, samples, affiliate program on an editor network (Skimlinks, Impact).
- **Why:** Commerce journalism is affiliate-funded and runs on a fixed calendar, so on-time, affiliate-ready pitches with samples win repeatably.
- **Do:** 1) join an editor-used network; 2) calendar: print June–July, national online Aug–Sept, bloggers Oct, plus Mother's/Father's Day and Valentine's; 3) target last year's round-ups and writers; 4) 5-line pitch: price, who it's for, detail, affiliate status, sample; 5) one follow-up at 4–5 days; 6) re-pitch yearly.
- **Expect:** 2–8% pitch-to-placement (100 pitches → 2–8) [practitioner estimate]; affiliate access near-required [nicolepajer.substack.com]; sales very skewed, backlinks and "as seen in" last [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** samples + 10–15 h/season (vs $2–5k agency); 1–3 mo.
- **Track:** reply rate ≥10%; sample requests; guides in production.
- **Rules & risks:** never pay for editorial without a sponsored label; avoid pay-to-play guides.
- **ClimbOS does:** finds guides and writers, calendar, pitches, tracking. Owner: approve, ship.

### Subscribe-and-save replenishment (`subscribe-and-save-replenishment`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** consumables (coffee, tea, snacks, supplements, pet food, skincare, refills) with a 2–12 wk use-up cycle.
- **Why:** Replenishment retains very well, so subscriptions multiply LTV and raise the CPA every other play can afford.
- **Do:** 1) median days-to-reorder per SKU; 2) default to it, offer ±1 step; 3) 10–15% off plus a non-price perk; 4) A/B a visible pre-selected toggle; 5) "running low?" email at 80% of reorder time; 6) easy skip and pause.
- **Expect:** second-box retention 92–96% in pet food, supplements, coffee vs 30–40% loss in beauty boxes [eightx.co]; churn ~6.5–7.1%/mo [eightx.co]; adoption 10–30% of replenishable orders [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** app $0–300/mo; 6–10 h; 30–60 days.
- **Track:** subscription checkout rate; skip vs cancel; running-low conversion.
- **Rules & risks:** LEGAL REVIEW. FTC click-to-cancel vacated July 2025, but ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws (e.g. California) need disclosure, consent, easy cancel.
- **ClimbOS does:** reorder analysis, PDP copy, flows, tests, win-backs. Owner: app, discounts.

### Product-finder quiz as lead capture (`product-finder-quiz`) · evidence weak · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** DTC with ≥6 SKUs and confusing choice (skin type, taste, fit); coaching program fit.
- **Why:** A personalised answer earns more opt-ins than a discount pop-up, and answers become segmentation data.
- **Do:** 1) 4–7 questions mapped to SKUs; 2) show results either way, optional "email me"; 3) pass answers to ESP; 4) 3-email result-specific flow; 5) entry in nav, hero and ads; 6) cut worst drop-off question monthly.
- **Expect:** vendor ceilings: quiz-taker CVR 7–25% vs 2–3% site, opt-in 42–75%, Geologie 9%→13% [octaneai.com], unverified [airframe.ai]; plan 25–45% opt-in, 3–8% buy in 30 days [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–200/mo or in-house; ~8 h; 2 wks.
- **Track:** start rate; completion; opt-in on completion.
- **Rules & risks:** no diagnostic health claims; consent; GDPR and CCPA cover answers.
- **ClimbOS does:** logic, questions, quiz on our page builder, flows, analysis. Owner: approve mappings.

### Google Merchant Center free listings (`merchant-center-free-listings`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 2
- **Fits:** any DTC brand or maker with its own store and a feed.
- **Why:** Google shows unpaid product listings, many small stores lack a clean feed, and feed quality decides visibility.
- **Do:** 1) connect via Google & YouTube app; 2) fix every disapproval; 3) titles = Brand + type + key attribute + size/colour; 4) add review feed, sale price, shipping speed; 5) monthly rewrite low-CTR titles.
- **Expect:** at launch ~2× views and +50% clicks with ads, SMBs most [chainstoreage.com]; agencies saw +5.6% to +10% clicks [ppchero.com]; a modest durable floor, not a spike.
- **Cost / time:** $0; 3–6 h then 1 h/mo; 1–3 wks.
- **Track:** approved share; unpaid impressions; unpaid CTR.
- **Rules & risks:** price and stock must match landing page or suspension; health restrictions.
- **ClimbOS does:** feed audit, titles, attributes, monthly report. Owner: connect, verify.

### App Store product page A/B tests (`app-store-page-tests`) · evidence moderate · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** iOS apps with ≥3k page views/mo (Google Play has equivalents).
- **Why:** Installs hinge on the first 2–3 screenshots, Apple tests 3 treatments natively, and gains compound across sources.
- **Do:** 1) first screenshot shows the outcome, not UI; 2) one element per test; 3) ≥7 days each; 4) localise top 3 non-English markets; 5) check day-7 retention of cohorts; 6) custom pages per ad audience.
- **Expect:** Headspace +34% organic conversion [mcsaatchiperformance.com]; ŠKODA +15% from iPad screenshots [splitmetrics.com]; realistic 0–10% per test, 1 in 3 wins [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0 + design; ~4 h/test; 1–3 wks.
- **Track:** conversion by variant; day-7 retention.
- **Rules & risks:** screenshots must reflect the app (2.3); no medical claims.
- **ClimbOS does:** variants in our editor, captions, localisation, test queue, analysis. Owner: upload.

### Engagement-timed in-app review prompt (`in-app-review-prompt-timing`) · evidence weak · predictability 5 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** apps with < ~500 ratings or < 4.5 average, habit or health apps with clear wins.
- **Why:** Apple allows 3 prompts per 365 days, so asking right after a success moment selects happy users.
- **Do:** 1) define 2–3 success moments; 2) call native API only there; 3) optional "Enjoying X?" pre-filter, check current guidance; 4) reply to negatives within 48h; 5) A/B test the moment.
- **Expect:** anecdotes only: one app ~1.5→~4.7 stars [appbot.co]; +50% five-stars claim without method [clevertap.com]; plan 2–5× more ratings and +0.2–0.5 stars [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** 1–2 dev days; 1–2 wks.
- **Track:** ratings per week; average new rating; private-feedback share.
- **Rules & risks:** Apple controls display; never incentivise ratings.
- **ClimbOS does:** moments, copy, review replies, trends. Developer: implement.

### JV affiliate launch (`jv-affiliate-launch`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** courses or coaching with a proven $200–2,000 offer and 5–20 non-competing partners.
- **Why:** 85–95% margins fund 40–50% commissions, and partner lists concentrate sales into one launch week.
- **Do:** 1) prove EPC on own list; 2) recruit 5k–100k-audience partners; 3) 40–50% commission, 30–90 day cookie; 4) swipe kit: 3 emails, 2 posts, bonus; 5) leaderboard prizes; 6) pay after refund window.
- **Expect:** commissions 30–50%, JV 40–50% [learnybox.com]; 30% on a 3–4% converter beats 50% on a dud [teachery.co]; 20% of partners drive 80%, 30–50% promote at all [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** commission only; 20–40 h; 6–10 wks.
- **Track:** partner sends; registrations per partner; EPC.
- **Rules & risks:** LEGAL REVIEW for earnings claims (FTC enforcement); partner disclosure; 1099s.
- **ClimbOS does:** partner finding, pitches, swipe kit, bonus pages, leaderboard, reports. Owner: calls, payouts.

### 5-day challenge funnel (`five-day-challenge`) · evidence weak · predictability 3 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** coaches, courses, communities, habit apps; offer $27–997.
- **Why:** A visible 5-day win builds trust faster than a lead magnet, and the day 4–5 pitch reaches people who did the work.
- **Do:** 1) one 5-day outcome; 2) registration page, daily email, community space; 3) daily 30–45 min session, one task; 4) pitch day 4–5, 48–72h deadline; 5) fill via boosts, JV partners, own list; 6) repeat quarterly.
- **Expect:** vendor 10–30% of active participants buy, optimistic [passion.io]; better anchor: registration→purchase >3%, day-5 completion >30% [openskillindex.com, unverified]; 1,000 registrants → ~20–50 sales under $500 [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–100 + ads; ~20 h/run; 3–4 wks.
- **Track:** registration conversion; day-1 show-up; % active day 4.
- **Rules & risks:** LEGAL REVIEW for earnings and health claims; TCPA for SMS.
- **ClimbOS does:** page, daily messages, task sheets, pitch script, tracking. Owner: host.

### Live workshop/webinar sell (`live-workshop-sell`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** coaches and courses selling $300–3,000; complex consumer products (e.g. espresso class).
- **Why:** Live teaching handles objections, and documented show-up and buy rates make revenue forecastable.
- **Do:** 1) title around a specific result; 2) reminders at 24h, 1h, start; 3) 40 min teach, 15 min offer, 48h bonus; 4) 48h replay with deadline emails; 5) track live and replay separately.
- **Expect:** show-up 47.7% across 33,786 sessions [livestorm.co], 21–33% B2B [goldcast.io]; attendee→buyer 5–15% [ruzuku.com], up to 10–20% [learnybox.com]; 500 registrants → ~8–30 sales [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–100/mo; 6–10 h/run; 2–3 wks.
- **Track:** registrations; live attendance; % staying to offer.
- **Rules & risks:** LEGAL REVIEW for earnings claims; real deadlines; recording consent.
- **ClimbOS does:** page, reminders, outline, offer script, replay flow. Owner: present.

### Niche-publisher affiliate program (`niche-publisher-affiliate-program`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** DTC, makers, apps with ≥40% GM after commission; prerequisite for seeding, gift guides, YouTube.
- **Why:** An always-on commission pays every publisher who mentions you only when a sale happens.
- **Do:** 1) Shopify Collabs (free, 2.9% payout fee, $25 min) or tiered app; 2) 10–20%, more for new customers; 3) recruit 50 publishers/mo; 4) starter kit; 5) first-sale bonus in 30 days; 6) prune quarterly, raise top 10%.
- **Expect:** 10–20% of recruits ever sell, 1–3% drive most revenue; mature programs 5–15% of DTC revenue [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0 + commission; 4–6 h/wk; 3–8 wks.
- **Track:** recruited→first click→first sale; clicks per active affiliate.
- **Rules & risks:** disclosure; attribution fraud alleged in a 2026 Collabs review [apps.shopify.com]; coupon leakage; ban brand bidding.
- **ClimbOS does:** finds "best [category]" publishers, recruitment, kits, nudges. Owner: approve rates.

### Micro-YouTuber review outreach (`micro-youtuber-review-outreach`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** considered purchases searched as "X review"; product must survive honest review.
- **Why:** Reviews rank for years, and sub-70k channels are rarely sponsored (6.6% at ~71k subs), so they are under-pitched.
- **Do:** 1) mine "[category] review", "[competitor] vs" for 2k–70k channels with views ≥20% of subs; 2) rank by fit; 3) free product for honest review, or pay by average views; 4) never script the verdict; 5) affiliate link; 6) track views at 30/90/365 days.
- **Expect:** 5–15% agree to free reviews; 0.2–1% of views click [practitioner estimate]; sales accrue 12–36 months.
- **Cost / time:** product, or $50–300/video for 1k–10k channels [bluehost.com]; CPM $15–40 [collabpals.com]; 3–8 wks.
- **Track:** agreement rate; day-30 views; affiliate clicks per 1k views.
- **Rules & risks:** paid-promotion box plus spoken disclosure, even for free product; reviews may be negative.
- **ClimbOS does:** channel mining, pitches, rate estimates, briefs, tracking. Owner: approve, ship.

### Pay-per-new-customer networks (`pay-per-new-customer-networks`) · evidence weak · predictability 3 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** Shopify DTC in US/Canada, AOV ≥$35, known max CAC.
- **Why:** You pay only for new customers under your CPA cap (Shop Campaigns; Disco on partner thank-you pages).
- **Do:** 1) max CPA = first-order margin + 60% of 6-month repeat margin; 2) Shop Campaigns: cap, daily budget ≥1× CPA; 3) Disco: complementary partners, offer tile; 4) compare 60-day repeat; 5) scale where 90-day LTV:CAC >1.5.
- **Expect:** vendor cases, mostly mid-size: Kizik 13k+ customers at >3× return, Firebelly Tea 2× ROAS [shopify.com]; small stores ~5–15% of new customers [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** CPA only; Disco fee + CPA, verify [carthook.com]; 2–3 h; 1–2 wks.
- **Track:** pacing vs cap; new-customer CPA; 60-day repeat.
- **Rules & risks:** cannibalisation; deal-seekers; thank-you page clutter.
- **ClimbOS does:** CPA cap, tiles, cohort analysis. Owner: enrol, fund.

### Package-insert swaps and insert networks (`package-insert-swaps`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** DTC shipping ≥300 orders/mo; paid networks need $25k+.
- **Why:** An opened box is a high-attention moment with a qualified buyer, and 1:1 swaps cost only printing.
- **Do:** 1) 10 complementary brands, similar volume; 2) 60-day equal swap, unique code and QR; 3) one-offer postcard; 4) track per partner; 5) renew partners > 0.5% redemption; 6) graduate to a 10k–25k paid test.
- **Expect:** paid inserts ~$0.05–0.08 vs $0.40–0.60 direct mail, "8× ROAS often" agency claim; brands testing inserts +100% 2023→2024 [modernretail.co]; swap redemption 0.3–1.5% [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0.03–0.10/card; paid $25–50k/mo [modernretail.co]; ~3 h/partner; 3–6 wks.
- **Track:** QR scans per 1k inserts; redemptions; survey mentions.
- **Rules & risks:** partner quality; food packaging; GDPR if data shared.
- **ClimbOS does:** partners, proposals, postcards, codes, tracking. Owner: approve, print.

### Faire wholesale plus Faire Direct (`faire-wholesale-plus-direct`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** makers and DTC profitable at 50% of retail, able to fill 20–200-unit orders.
- **Why:** Faire reaches 700k+ retailers and Faire Direct invites carry 0% commission; each stockist is a showroom.
- **Do:** 1) wholesale 50% of MSRP, $100–200 minimums; 2) invite 200 boutiques stocking similar brands via Faire Direct; 3) sample box to top 20; 4) shelf-talker after first order; 5) reorder nudge at 45–60 days; 6) list stockists on site.
- **Expect:** modelled reorder 20–35% year one, 60% mature; Direct margin ~38% vs 14–18% blended [eightx.co]; "$5–20k/mo in 6–12 months" is anecdotal [evolveamz.com]; boutique outbound → first order 3–8% [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** 15% + $10 first orders (NA), 25% non-NA, 15% reorders, 0% Direct [eightx.co]; ~5 h/wk; 2–6 wks.
- **Track:** Direct link clicks; sample-box conversion; 60-day reorders.
- **Rules & risks:** brand pays retailer free returns; keep price parity.
- **ClimbOS does:** boutique finding, invites, sample letters, shelf-talkers, reorder nudges. Owner: approve, ship.

### Amazon pre-launch Vine (`amazon-vine-launch`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** brand-registered Amazon sellers launching a tested, differentiated product.
- **Why:** Vine is Amazon's sanctioned honest-review route and since June 2025 can run pre-launch, so ads don't hit a 0-review page.
- **Do:** 1) finish images, A+ and title; 2) enrol 10–30 units pre-launch; 3) fix issues reviewers flag; 4) Sponsored Products only at 5+ reviews; 5) add tagged external traffic.
- **Expect:** 60–90% of units reviewed in 6 weeks [practitioner estimate]; conversion follows the 0→5 review curve.
- **Cost / time:** $0/$75/$200 per parent ASIN for 2/10/30 units [bqool.com]; true cost 2–4× fee [novadata.io]; 2–6 wks.
- **Track:** units claimed; reviews and stars; listing conversion.
- **Rules & risks:** negative Vine reviews stay; never contact reviewers or solicit off-Amazon.
- **ClimbOS does:** listing and A+ copy, review themes, fix list. Owner: enrolment, stock.
