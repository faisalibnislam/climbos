## Reference: footer and compliance content by country

Every cold message carries a footer below the signature, in plain text. Keep it to 2-4 short lines. You write the content; the system adds the one-click unsubscribe header and processes opt-outs immediately.

| Recipient country | Footer must contain | Opt-out deadline the sender must meet |
|---|---|---|
| United States (CAN-SPAM) | Real sender identity, the company's valid postal address, a working opt-out line. The message must be plainly commercial and the subject must not mislead | 10 business days (honoured immediately in practice) |
| United Kingdom (PECR, UK GDPR) | Sender identity, a valid address for opting out, and the Article 14 line in the first email | Immediately |
| EU countries allowed by the rules table (GDPR, national ePrivacy) | Sender identity, postal address, opt-out, Article 14 line in the first email | Immediately |
| Canada (CASL) | Sender identity, mailing address **plus** a phone number, email or web contact, and an unsubscribe line | 10 business days |
| Australia (Spam Act) | Accurate sender identity and a functional unsubscribe line | 5 business days |
| Germany, Austria; UK sole traders and partnerships; Canada or Australia with an inferred address | Do not draft. The prospect is blocked | n/a |

### Line patterns

- **Opt-out (all countries):** "Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't email again." or "If you'd rather not hear from me, reply 'stop' and I'll take you off my list."
- **Article 14 (UK and EU, first email):** "I found your work email on {source, e.g. your website's contact page}. How we handle your data: {sender domain}/privacy. Reply 'no' and I'll delete it." The privacy pointer is the only web address allowed in a step 1 email; write it as plain text on the sender's own domain. If the brief has no privacy notice URL, use `[NEEDS FACT: privacy notice URL]`.
- **Postal address:** the business address from confirmed facts, on one line. Never a made-up address. If missing, `[NEEDS FACT: postal address]`.
- **Signature:** real first name and surname, role, company. The display name must be a real person who works for or represents the business.

### Example footer (UK recipient)

> Sam Patel, Founder, Fernhill Web Studio
> 12 Example Street, Leeds LS1 0AA
> I found your work email on your website's contact page. How we handle data: fernhill.example/privacy. Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I'll delete it and won't email again.
