# Growth plays: B2B SaaS, prosumer/dev tools, B2B services

From `docs/research/plays/saas-b2b.md` (Oct 2026; teams of 1–20). Source rank order (predictability × leverage). Evidence grades are the source's (moderate = several agreeing practitioner reports; weak = anecdote, vendor claim or none); none is strong. 1–5 scores are editorial, from rank and evidence. Cold email: `docs/research/leadgen-cold-email.md`.

## How to use this catalog

- **Fit-gate first:** no Show HN without a no-signup demo, no review push under 10 happy customers, no Product Hunt for vertical B2B, no programmatic SEO without unique data.
- **Services firms:** promote 33 Clutch (top 5 for services), 19 CPE/CLE, 15 expert PR, 14 podcasts, 17 referral partnerships, 7 calculators/audits, 25 research. Demote Product Hunt, Show HN, AppSumo, Chrome, open source. Lawyers/CPAs: check advertising and referral-fee rules.
- **Dev tools:** favour 18 Show HN, 30 open source, 21 MCP directories, 35 hackathons, 3 integrations, 27 Reddit. **Prosumer/creator:** 9 Product Hunt, 12 YouTube, 13 affiliates, 22 Chrome, 23 templates; review sites (2) matter less. **Vertical B2B:** skip launches; favour 1, 2, 4, 5, 19.
- **Bundles:** switching = 1 + 6 + 20. AI visibility (8) is fed by 2, 27, 15 and the roundup from 1. A free tool (7) gives 9 and 18 something to launch. Creators from 11–12 seed affiliates (13). Partners (16) become summit speakers (32). Research (25) feeds 15 and 27.
- **Evidence caveat:** most figures are self-reports or from vendors selling the channel (Dupple, Rewardful, UserGems, Crossbeam, Zapier). Plan on the low half, show grades and estimate labels, never promise vendor numbers.
- **2026 context:** G2 owns Capterra, Software Advice, GetApp (Feb 2026), concentrating risk. AI engines cite third-party lists and Reddit. LinkedIn TLAs boost non-employee posts. ChatGPT app directory is open.
- **Human in the loop:** for Show HN, Reddit, PR, podcasts and creator posts the owner posts or approves personally; ClimbOS never auto-posts or impersonates.

## Plays

### 1. Competitor alternatives / "vs" pages + switching kit (`competitor-alternatives-pages`) · evidence moderate (vendor-sourced) · predictability 4 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** SaaS with 1–10 searched competitors; services vs a model ("in-house vs agency"). Needs a real differentiator, ≥3 switchers.
- **Why:** "[X] alternative/vs" searchers are mid-purchase and unhappy; honest pages catch them for years.
- **Do:** 1) size alternative/vs terms, interview 3–5 switchers; 2) one intent per page: TL;DR table, where they win, who shouldn't switch, dated pricing, quote; 3) switching kit + free migration (6); 4) fair roundup (feeds 8); 5) recheck quarterly.
- **Expect:** 8.43% avg conversion, 95 articles [eesel.ai]; 7.5%+ [tripledart.com]; best cases 23% to trial, €500k ARR from one page [founderpath.com]. Plan 1–5% signup, 50–2,000 visits/mo per niche page [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–1.5k/page or 6–10 h first, 3–5 h after; organic 4–12 wks [practitioner estimate].
- **Track:** GSC position; page signup rate.
- **Rules & risks:** truthful, substantiated claims (FTC Act §5); no logos implying endorsement; ad trademark limits.
- **ClimbOS does:** keyword research, pricing scrape, page drafts, recheck alerts. Owner: differentiators, quotes, approve.

### 2. Review-velocity campaign on G2/Capterra (`review-velocity-campaign`) · evidence moderate · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** B2B SaaS with ≥10 happy active customers (agencies: 33); low value for prosumer.
- **Why:** buyers and AI read review sites; grids reward count and recency, so small vendors can lead niches.
- **Do:** 1) narrowest winnable category; 2) invite ALL active customers in a segment, G2-approved gift cards; 3) 3-touch founder/CSM ask + in-app prompt after wins; 4) reply within 48 h; 5) push before G2 quarterly cutoffs.
- **Expect:** 7→107 reviews in a quarter [reachdesk.com]. 5–20% review rate with incentive, 1–5% without; 0–10 direct leads/mo [practitioner estimate]. Capterra PPC from $2/click [team4.agency].
- **Cost / time:** $0–2.5k (cards $10–50); 10–20 h over 4–8 wks; reviews 1–2 wks, badges next quarter.
- **Track:** submission/approval rate; category rank.
- **Rules & risks:** FTC rule (16 CFR 465): no fake/bought or positive-only incentives, ~$51–53k/violation [natlawreview.com]; no review-gating; no staff/friends [g2.com].
- **ClimbOS does:** segment, invite sequences, cutoff reminders, reply drafts. Owner: approve full list, fund, CSM asks.

### 3. Zapier/Make integration + template pages (`zapier-integration-templates`) · evidence moderate (vendor-curated) · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** B2B/prosumer SaaS with an API or webhooks; a developer for 1–3 weeks.
- **Why:** Zapier pair pages rank for "[A] + [B]" searches; integrations cut churn.
- **Do:** 1) public integration, 3–5 triggers/actions; 2) 10–30 templates for top pairs; 3) embed templates in-app; 4) mirror on Make/n8n; 5) own /integrations/[tool] pages.
- **Expect:** embeds +40% integration signups; tl;dv +20% active users; Jotform Zapier users 25x likelier to upgrade [zapier.com]. ~1–5% of new signups via directory; main gain retention [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0; 40–120 dev h + 10 h copy; live 2–6 wks, traffic 1–3 mo.
- **Track:** active Zaps; Zapier-referred signups.
- **Rules & risks:** broken integrations get de-listed; platform terms; API costs.
- **ClimbOS does:** workflows from tickets, specs, template/page copy. Owner: build, maintain, submit.

### 4. Platform app marketplaces (HubSpot, Slack, Shopify, Atlassian, Xero/QBO) (`platform-app-marketplaces`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** SaaS whose buyers live in one platform; a real in-workflow integration.
- **Why:** marketplace users are pre-qualified and searching; rank rewards reviews and installs.
- **Do:** 1) ONE platform; 2) hit thresholds via customers (HubSpot ≥3 installs + case study; Slack ≥10 active installs from Sep 2026); 3) app-store-style listing + 60-s video; 4) 10+ reviews in 30 days; 5) partner program.
- **Expect:** new Shopify app 0–2 installs/wk [indiehackers.com]; optimised app #42→#11, ~50–60→100+ installs/mo in 3–6 mo [appjubilee.io]. "Can't rely on being listed" [hubspot.com]. Atlassian ~$1.8B 2024, skewed [aventis-advisors.com].
- **Cost / time:** $0–5k (Atlassian ~25% take); 80–300 dev h; approval 2–8 wks, rank 4–12 wks.
- **Track:** view→install rate; reviews last 30 days.
- **Rules & risks:** policy changes; security review; Slack de-lists stale apps; review incentives usually banned.
- **ClimbOS does:** platform pick, keyword research, listing copy, rank tracking. Owner: build, secure, support.

### 5. Champion and alumni tracking (`champion-alumni-tracking`) · evidence moderate (vendor-sourced) · predictability 4 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** B2B SaaS/services with ≥50 past/present users or client contacts in a CRM.
- **Why:** ~20% of champions change jobs yearly [usergems.com]; a fan with new budget is the warmest lead.
- **Do:** 1) export everyone with good outcomes; 2) monthly job-change check (LinkedIn alerts or enrichment; UserGems ~$40–150k/yr [leadmagic.io]); 3) founder note within 30 days + free transfer/pilot; 4) alert the old account; 5) own pipeline tag.
- **Expect:** ex-customers ~5% of closed-won; ~3x likelier to buy; SkillSurvey 80% on first 46 opps [usergems.com]; 12% vs <2% cold (unverified) [leadmagic.io]. 200 champions → ~40 moves → 4–10 conversations/yr [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–200/mo; 2–4 h/mo; 2–6 wks.
- **Track:** moves detected; reply rate.
- **Rules & risks:** legitimately collected data; GDPR LIA; no LinkedIn scraping; one-to-one only.
- **ClimbOS does:** list upkeep, move detection, note drafts. Owner: send each note, take calls.

### 6. Free concierge migration (`free-migration-switch-offer`) · evidence weak–moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** SaaS vs an entrenched incumbent where switching is the objection; firms taking over a provider.
- **Why:** removing migration effort turns "later" into "this month" and gives ads a hook.
- **Do:** 1) 1–2 competitors whose exports you import; 2) offer "free migration in X days or month free" + parallel run; 3) promote on pages (1), ads, technographic outbound; 4) optional contract match (SugarCRM: 50% off + migration); 5) cap monthly.
- **Expect:** Lemkin calls it "magic" [saastr.com]; no win-rate data. Maybe +10–30% relative close rate on competitive deals [practitioner estimate]; A/B it.
- **Cost / time:** $0; 2–20 h/migration; importer 20–80 dev h; 1–4 wks.
- **Track:** close rate with vs without offer; 90-day retention.
- **Rules & risks:** no misleading trademark use; you're a data processor (DPA); cap volume.
- **ClimbOS does:** find competitor users, offer page/ads, runbook. Owner: run migrations, set cap.

### 7. Free tool or calculator (`engineering-as-marketing-free-tool`) · evidence moderate · predictability 3 · leverage 5
- **Fits:** SaaS, dev tools, services (ROI calculator, estimator, audit, grader).
- **Why:** free tools earn search, links and shares better than posts and lead into the product.
- **Do:** 1) mine calls/tickets/keywords for repeat questions; 2) pick one with demand, <2-wk build, adjacent to paid value; 3) ungated, soft CTA at output; 4) launch on PH, Show HN, subreddits; 5) pitch "best free tools" lists.
- **Expect:** VenturePact's cost calculator became its top lead source [microconf.com]; HubSpot Grader reached millions [theventurecrew.substack.com]. 500–10,000 visits/mo after 6 mo, 1–5% leads [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–3k; 20–80 h; launch days, search 2–6 mo.
- **Track:** email capture; tool-to-trial.
- **Rules & risks:** rate-limit AI/API costs; privacy notice.
- **ClimbOS does:** question mining, spec/build simple tools, launch posts. Owner: approve, check, host.

### 8. Get onto list pages AI engines cite (`ai-answer-listicle-inclusion`) · evidence moderate (new field) · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** B2B SaaS/services where buyers ask AI "best [category] for [use case]".
- **Why:** AI answers cite third-party lists and Reddit, not vendor sites.
- **Do:** 1) run 20–50 buyer prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews; log cited URLs; 2) list cited pages + authors; 3) pitch data, free account, quote or correction; 4) own list-style roundup (1); 5) re-run monthly.
- **Expect:** ChatGPT cited the tool's own site 11.6%, G2/Capterra 0.9%; all cited pages were lists [derivatex.agency]; Reddit top cited domain [gracker.ai]; ~40% of cited lists vendor-written [exposureninja.com]. 2–8 inclusions/quarter from 30–60 pitches [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–2k; 10–20 h then 4–8 h/mo; 1–3 mo.
- **Track:** mention rate across prompts; cited pages including you.
- **Rules & risks:** paid placements must be disclosed; no fake "independent" sites.
- **ClimbOS does:** prompt runs, citation logs, author contacts, pitches. Owner: approve, decide paid inclusion.

### 9. Product Hunt launch (`product-hunt-launch`) · evidence moderate (self-reported) · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** prosumer, AI, dev, horizontal B2B with self-serve trial; 200+ supporters. Weak for vertical B2B/services.
- **Why:** one day concentrates early adopters and curators; top 5 brings traffic, links, badge.
- **Do:** 1) 3–4 wks active on PH + supporter list; 2) Tue–Thu hardest, weekends easier but less traffic; 3) tagline <60 chars, 30–60-s video, 4–6 images, maker comment; 4) post 12:01 AM PT, personal account; ask feedback not upvotes; 5) repurpose 90 days.
- **Expect:** survey: 24.5% got 100–500 users, 26.5% 500–1k, 22.4% 1–2k [mysignature.io]. #2 of day: 140 signups, 22 paid [dev.to]. Top 5: 500–3,000 visitors, 50–300 signups dev/prosumer [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $0–500; 30–60 h over 4 wks; same day.
- **Track:** comments/hour; PH signup activation.
- **Rules & risks:** no bought votes, rings or "please upvote" (de-featuring); company accounts can't post.
- **ClimbOS does:** fit check, asset copy, feedback DMs, reply drafts. Owner: video, post and reply personally.

### 10. LinkedIn creator posts → Thought Leader Ads (`linkedin-creator-thought-leader-ads`) · evidence moderate (ads), weak (pipeline) · predictability 3 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** B2B SaaS/services selling to LinkedIn-active roles; $1k+ test budget.
- **Why:** buyers trust practitioners; TLAs boost creator posts (with permission) at low CPC.
- **Do:** 1) 10–30 creators matching buyer role (5k–50k often best value); 2) verify ICP commenters, not pods; 3) honest post with partnership label + TLA rights; 4) run 3–5, boost winner to company list + titles; 5) boost founder posts too.
- **Expect:** TLA median CTR 2.68%, CPC $2.29 vs image 0.42%, $13.23 [zenabm.com]. Creator median ~$798/post [favikon.com]; $250–4,500 for 5k–100k tech [launchpointhq.com]. Cost per demo $150–600 [practitioner estimate].
- **Cost / time:** $1.5–8k first test; 10–20 h; 1–3 wks.
- **Track:** ICP-title comments; creator-UTM demos.
- **Rules & risks:** FTC disclosure with the endorsement; "brand partnership" label; no own headline on TLAs.
- **ClimbOS does:** find/score creators, price check, briefs. Owner: approve, sign, pay, run ads.

### 11. Newsletter sponsorship test-buys (`newsletter-sponsorship-tests`) · evidence moderate (publisher-sourced) · predictability 4 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** B2B, dev, creator SaaS and services with a niche; offer page, $500–3k.
- **Why:** rent trusted niche audiences at known prices; small lists engage more.
- **Do:** 1) list 20–50 newsletters (ask customers; beehiiv, Substack, Paved, Swapstack); 2) compare price per unique click; 3) 3–5 small native placements, per-list page; 4) UTMs/codes, judge paid at 30/60 days; 5) rebook winners.

- **Rules & risks:** "sponsored" labels; bot opens (Apple MPP); creative fees.
- **ClimbOS does:** discover/score lists, ads, pages, attribution. Owner: approve, pay.

### 13. Affiliate program seeded with customers/creators (`affiliate-program-seeded`) · evidence moderate (vendor dataset) · predictability 3 · leverage 4
- **Fits:** prosumer, creator, SMB, AI SaaS with self-serve checkout ≥$15/mo (enterprise: 17).
- **Why:** pay only on results; a few motivated affiliates drive most revenue.
- **Do:** 1) 20–30% recurring for 12 months, 60–90-day cookie; 2) tracking (Rewardful etc.); 3) invite top 20 customers, creators from 11–12, bloggers; 4) asset kit and codes; 5) monthly 1:1 with top 5, fraud audit.

- **Cost / time:** $50–150/mo + commission; 15 h, then 4–8 h/mo; 1–3 mo.
- **Track:** active affiliates; paid conversions.
- **Rules & risks:** FTC disclosure; ban brand PPC and coupon spam; cap duration.
- **ClimbOS does:** find candidates, outreach, kit, fraud flags. Owner: terms, payouts.

### 15. Expert-source PR (Qwoted, Featured, SOS) (`expert-source-pr`) · evidence moderate (vendor tests) · predictability 3 · leverage 3
- **Fits:** any business with an expert who answers within hours; strong for accountants, lawyers, consultants.
- **Why:** journalists ask for quotes daily; fast answers earn links and AI-visible mentions.
- **Do:** 1) join Featured (owns HARO), Qwoted (Pro ~$99/mo), SOS, Help a B2B Writer; 2) filter 5–10 expert topics; 3) reply in 2–6 h: quotable lead, data point, credential; 4) keep approved bios/stats; 5) build a 20-journalist list.
- **Expect:** Qwoted 70.3% high-authority queries, ~6% approval; SOS 36.26% followed links [blckalpaca.at]. 2–6 placements per 50 pitches [practitioner estimate]; few direct leads.
- **Cost / time:** $0–100/mo; 3–5 h/wk; 2–8 wks.
- **Track:** acceptance rate; followed links.
- **Rules & risks:** genuine expert views only (Featured detects AI); professional rules; "as featured in" only if real.
- **ClimbOS does:** monitor feeds, draft in expert's voice, track. Owner: approve and personalise each.

### 23. Templates in ecosystem galleries (`ecosystem-template-galleries`) · evidence weak–moderate · predictability 2 · leverage 3
