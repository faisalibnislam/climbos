Fictional business: **Brightwater Boilers**, a three-engineer boiler repair firm in Leeds. Page audited: the Google Ads landing page. Confirmed facts: £79 diagnostic visit deducted from any repair, Gas Safe registered, same-day slots when booked before 11am, 212 Google reviews at 4.8. Details are invented for illustration.

### Good (excerpt)

> **Context:** paid landing page; job = book a diagnostic visit; traffic = Google Search ads on "boiler repair Leeds" (ad headline: "Same-Day Boiler Repair in Leeds"); stage = problem-aware, urgent.
>
> **Top 3**
> 1. **Blocker, message mismatch.** H1 reads "Heating Solutions You Can Trust". The ad promised same-day repair; the page never says "same-day" above the form. Fix: H1 "Same-day boiler repair in Leeds"; sub "Book before 11am and an engineer visits today. £79 diagnostic, taken off your repair bill." Impact (hypothesis): more ad clickers start the form because the page confirms the promise they clicked. Verify: form starts / ad clicks, 3 weeks before vs after (directional). Effort: 30 min.
> 2. **Major, hidden price.** £79 appears only in the FAQ. VoC: 9 of 64 competitor reviews mention surprise call-out fees. Fix: price chip under the CTA: "£79 diagnostic, deducted from any repair." Effort: 15 min.
> 3. **Major, tracking.** No conversion event on the form in the page source. Fix: add a form-submit conversion and import booked jobs as offline conversions. Without this no other fix can be verified.
>
> **Not verified:** mobile layout and load speed (no render or PageSpeed data). Request a mobile PageSpeed Insights report; check LCP ≤ 2.5 s and INP ≤ 200 ms.

**Why it's good:** context drives the findings, each fix quotes the problem, cites evidence, supplies exact copy from confirmed facts, labels impact as a hypothesis with a verification metric, and flags what wasn't observed.

### Bad

> The page looks outdated and loads slowly. Add more social proof, a stronger CTA and urgency like a countdown timer. Add testimonials such as "Best boiler company in Leeds! – John D." Industry average conversion is 6.6%, so these changes should double your conversions. Also consider a chatbot, a blog and a new logo.

**Why it's bad:** speed and looks claimed without observation, generic tips, a fake-urgency dark pattern, an invented testimonial, a promised lift from a cross-industry average, and no ranking or exact fixes.
