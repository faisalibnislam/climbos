## Reference: what each money page must answer

| Page type | The buyer's question | Must be on the page | Common leaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid landing page | "Is this the thing the ad promised, and what do I do now?" | H1 repeating the ad promise; one offer; one CTA; proof chip; price or "from" price if the ad mentions it; navigation removed or minimised | H1 unrelated to the ad; homepage used as landing page; several CTAs; long form |
| Homepage | "What is this, is it for me, where do I go?" | Category and outcome in the H1; who it's for; the primary path plus 1–2 secondary paths; proof near the top | Slogan headline; "we"-centred copy; equal-weight menu of everything |
| Pricing page | "Which plan is for me, and what will I really pay?" | Plans in the buyer's terms (team size, volume); what's included; the anchor plan marked; billing and cancellation FAQs; risk reversal | "Contact us" with no guidance; feature walls; surprise add-ons; unclear annual vs monthly |
| Booking or quote page (local service) | "Do they cover my area, roughly what will it cost, and can they come soon?" | Areas served; price guidance or call-out fee if the owner confirms; next availability or response time; phone with click-to-call; short form; reviews with source | Phone-only; long form; no area list; no price signal; no response-time promise |
| Product page (ecommerce) | "Is it right for me, and what if it isn't?" | Real photos in use; price; delivery time and cost; returns policy near the button; reviews with the trade-offs; variant guidance (size, scent) | Shipping cost revealed at checkout; returns hidden; only studio shots; no size or fit help |

**Proof hierarchy (strongest first):** verifiable number tied to a named source → named customer quote with permission → demonstration or mechanism → guarantee or risk reversal → credentials or awards → unspecific superlatives (not proof; remove unless sourced).

**CRO checklist (paid landing pages):** the ad headline's words appear in the H1; a visitor can say what's offered, to whom and at what price within 5 seconds; one primary action; the form asks only what's needed now; proof sits next to each claim; copy at grade 5–8; mobile-first with the CTA in the first screen and tap targets ≥ 44 px; LCP and INP measured; no invented testimonials.

**Benchmarks:** Unbounce's 2024 data puts the cross-industry median landing-page conversion rate at 6.6%, and pages written at grade 5–7 converted at roughly double the median of college-level pages. Use these as context only. Judge this page against its own baseline, never against a cross-industry median.
