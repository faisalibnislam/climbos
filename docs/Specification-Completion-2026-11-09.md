## Focused tabs and AI-led owner experience

The owner selected Focused tabs. This is now implemented in the application, not only in the comparison prototype.

- Four main destinations: Home, Plan, AI activity and Results. Specialist routes remain discoverable under More and Tools; existing deep links remain valid.
- Home leads with saved advice. **Make it happen** prepares the saved move through the existing scoped request workflow and opens its actual work. It does not authorize publication, sending or spending.
- Plan opens on **Next steps**: the bigger goal, 14/30/90-day controls, one suggested move and small milestones. **Why this plan** holds strategy, alternatives, forecasts, research and detailed controls. **What we learned** holds reviews and version history. Critical refresh/review states remain visible.
- AI activity opens on **In progress**, with **Ready for you** and **Tools** beside it. Results separates **What happened**, **Where it came from** and **Look ahead**. Shared tabs have keyboard support and readable active/inactive states; scrollable tables accept keyboard focus.
- New onboarding defaults to 14 days and offers 14, 30 or 90. It starts with goal choices and keeps editable wording, cycle, budget and review-day controls optional and collapsed. Legacy saved horizons stay readable. Shorter cycles reduce scope rather than compressing a long forecast.
- Cycle length changes preserve the goal/budget and invalidate stale advice. Reviews use a single choice plus optional detail, save append-only history, start a new cycle and carry classified owner-recorded learning into the next writer. Unknown outcomes stay unknown. No job or external action starts merely because a review is saved.
- New plan milestones persist an exact day window, first action, how to check and continue/change/stop decision. Integration tests exercise 14- and 30-day generation and previous-review context. History is paginated across years and included in the bounded workspace export, with an explicit truncation flag.
- Climb AI now has an in-app `ask_owner` tool. It presents one necessary question with radio buttons or checkboxes, an unknown option and optional Other text. The tool loop stops for the answer. Answers are bound to the workspace, member and pending question, validated atomically with the reserved follow-up turn, and cannot be replayed as permission. Model-history context is separate from the short owner-facing receipt.
- AI instructions require reading available context before asking, taking initiative on safe preparation, explaining limitations and escalating only important unresolved choices. Prompt instructions do not establish that a model will always make good decisions.
