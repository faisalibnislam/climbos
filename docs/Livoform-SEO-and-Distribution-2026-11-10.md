# Livoform: use the useful work that already exists

Checked 10 November 2026. This is a local recommendation and draft review. No changes were made to Livoform, search properties, publishing accounts or recipients.

## The decision

Test whether a small agency finds the existing client-onboarding resource useful. The useful offer is a clearer client handoff, rather than another announcement that a form builder has AI. Connect one worked example, one social draft and an honest comparison to that same customer situation. Search demand, audience access and customer conversion are unknown.

The site already has a [client-onboarding kit](https://livoform.com/resources/client-onboarding-kit), a [detailed onboarding template](https://livoform.com/templates/client-onboarding) and a [Tally comparison](https://livoform.com/compare/tally). These pages were read, not inferred from a sitemap. Their described behavior is still a published promise; the app workflow has not been independently tested. Do not commission duplicates of these assets.

## What the SEO check actually found

Seven fetched pages — home, pricing, templates, use cases, Tally comparison, onboarding kit and onboarding template — each expose a title, description, one H1, a matching declared canonical, English language and a viewport declaration in static HTML. This sample gives no reason to make missing metadata the main recommendation. It does not establish Google indexing, rankings, speed, mobile usability or a whole-site audit. Search Console and measured query/traffic data were not supplied.

| Existing destination | Useful improvement | What remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Tally comparison | Separate a hosted Livoform link from a custom domain. Explain who should keep Tally. Date and source feature comparisons. | Regional and billing equivalence of displayed prices; actual switch intent. |
| Client-onboarding kit | Give an agency a small, concrete example to try with its own client handoff. Use the existing template as the next step. | Actual completion, usefulness and willingness to pay. |
| Client-onboarding template | Preserve the question-level detail. Verify the returning-client branch and distinguish free form logic from the paid AI reply before demonstrating it. | Tested behavior and owner willingness to support the proposed demonstration. |

The comparison currently puts a free Livoform-hosted handle beside Tally's paid custom domain. Those are different capabilities. [Tally's current pricing page](https://tally.so/pricing) also publishes free Notion/Airtable/Sheets integrations and puts custom domains, collaboration and partial submissions in Pro. Livoform should not present an integration that Tally supplies free as a paid advantage. The inspected Tally page displays EUR, while Livoform's comparison uses USD; do not calculate savings until the currency, region and billing basis match.

“Free client onboarding form template” and agency-specific comparison questions are query hypotheses, not measured keywords. Useful page improvements and internal links can proceed without invented search volumes. [Google's AI-feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) requires no special AI schema and promises no inclusion. Clear answers and source support serve readers first.

## Distribution with a reason

Use a proposed client-handoff demonstration rather than a broad feature tour. Show what the form asks, how a returning client sees a shorter path if that behavior is verified, and how a team agrees who signs off. Label the example as illustrative. Never present it as a real client result or a tested time saving.

For the first social test choose a single native format, such as a LinkedIn document or short narrated demonstration, aimed at agency owners managing client work. It should give readers a useful question or process they can apply even before switching tools. The AI-generated drafts in the local plan remain private and require review; no existing audience is assumed.

Named intermediaries need precise fit:

- [Matthias Frank's Tally/Notion tutorial](https://matthiasfrank.de/en/tally-with-notion/), edited 21 July 2026, is specific evidence of relevant form/workflow coverage. It also recommends Tally, so a generic switch pitch is weak. Contact policy, interest and placement terms remain unverified; keep this a research lead until those checks are complete.
- [Thomas Frank's About page](https://thomasjfrank.com/about/) invites business proposals and establishes Notion teaching, but it does not establish a strong agency-onboarding audience match. A convenient contact route alone should not make him the primary target.
- [Noxen Studio](https://www.noxenstudio.com/) now emphasizes bespoke systems and explicitly rejects prefab templates in its offer. A template-swap pitch based only on an older directory description would be poorly matched. Its customer booking form is not a verified promotional route.

G2 and Product Hunt can have later buyer-comparison or launch-feedback roles; neither supplies an assumed audience or an automatic weekly task. No review requests, incentivized reviews, outreach or public submissions were made.

## The small milestones

1. Within 14 days, review the prepared material, verify the exact demonstrated behavior and choose one permitted route. Record useful questions or observed attempts to try the resource. No replies or little traffic is inconclusive.
2. Around 30 days, improve the resource from observed friction. Add a second route only if it teaches something new; do not multiply pages and channels to fill a calendar.
3. Around 90 days, decide whether this agency use case deserves repeated investment. Over later years, verified examples and a record of real objections could become a useful library. They are not a forecast of growth.
