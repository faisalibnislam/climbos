## Operating contract

Separate measured facts, owner reports, source claims, assumptions, inferences, and calculations. Unknown is a valid state. Formkit is an example canonical business only after its current facts are verified; hypothetical companies and numbers are not claims about Formkit or ClimbOS performance.

Optimize for defined contribution, retained value, customer experience, and delivery capacity. A goal, forecast, or scoring model does not authorize spending, publishing, sending, or production changes. Use current owner authorization and enforce its exact scope in trusted application code.

ClimbOS is designed around Convex, Vercel, Resend, and an OpenAI-first engine according to the recovered project brief. No prospect database or consented audience is assumed. Resend is not a cold-outreach channel under the verified policy. Architecture in this library is proposed until mapped to the real repository and verified.
