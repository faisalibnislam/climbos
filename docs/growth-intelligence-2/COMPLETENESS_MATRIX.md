## What completion means

There are no placeholder chapters, “coming later” volumes, or starter outlines presented as completed books. Examples describe practical business situations, while invented numerical inputs and businesses are labeled hypothetical. The library provides actionable knowledge and proposed implementation contracts. It does not claim the ClimbOS engine was implemented or deployed during the writing project.
