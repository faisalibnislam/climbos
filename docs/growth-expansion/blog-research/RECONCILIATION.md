# Reconciling the blog research with the existing library

Edition 2026-10-20. Each entry records where the new sources disagree with each other, with the existing library (`docs/growth-intelligence/`, `docs/growth-expansion/`, `docs/growth-engine/book-review/`), or with current ClimbOS code. It names the conditions that decide which advice applies. The rules are proposed ClimbOS policy, not author endorsements. Finding IDs resolve in [source_registry.json](source_registry.json).

## Conditions that most often decide

- **Business model:** self-serve software vs services vs commerce. Activation, free-model and conversion-path advice applies to self-serve products; services keep their own milestones.
- **Data available:** owner counts, billing data, events. Without data, playbooks fall back to measuring first and never assume a benchmark.
- **Scale:** Microsoft- and venture-scale thresholds are labelled context and never used as small-business gates.
- **Interest:** sponsored or vendor-authored claims are treated as interested evidence.
