# ClimbOS — 1,000 Business Problems & Solution Playbooks
**Version:** 1.0 · **Created:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** Proposed knowledge base, not validated production rules

## Purpose and constraints
This library is a structured hypothesis catalog for ClimbOS, not ground truth, a model-training dataset with proven outcomes, or permission to alter a live system. Every problem is a possible symptom. Diagnose with actual evidence before selecting a tactic. Never fabricate results, reviews, customer identities, benchmarks, or certainty. Require owner approval before spending, publishing, messaging, or changing connected systems. Respect consent, privacy, local regulation and platform policies.

## Business type coverage
The 20 archetypes below cover common acquisition models. A business may map to multiple archetypes; classify by revenue model, buying journey, customer type, channel access and operational constraints—not industry label alone. Extend through subtypes, not an unbounded claim of “all possible businesses.”

1. **B2B SaaS** — workspace trials, demo requests and subscriptions
2. **Consumer SaaS** — self-serve signups and individual subscriptions
3. **Mobile and desktop apps** — store impressions, downloads and paid upgrades
4. **DTC e-commerce** — product-page visits, carts and completed orders
5. **Marketplaces** — buyer demand, seller supply and completed matches
6. **Restaurants and cafes** — local discovery, reservations, delivery and visits
7. **Hotels and hospitality** — searches, booking inquiries and occupied nights
8. **Professional services** — consultation requests, proposals and retained engagements
9. **Agencies and freelancers** — inbound briefs, discovery calls and project contracts
10. **B2B manufacturing and wholesale** — distributor inquiries, RFQs and repeat accounts
11. **Healthcare practices** — patient searches, appointment requests and attended visits
12. **Education and online courses** — course discovery, enrollment and learner completion
13. **Coaches and consultants** — audience interest, discovery calls and programs
14. **Real estate and property** — listing views, inquiries, showings and transactions
15. **Home and local services** — service-area searches, quote requests and booked jobs
16. **Nonprofits and membership organizations** — supporter visits, donations, volunteers and memberships
17. **Creators and media businesses** — content views, subscribers and paid memberships
18. **Events and ticketing** — event discovery, registrations and attendance
19. **Fintech and financial services** — product discovery, applications and verified accounts
20. **Enterprise software and complex sales** — account engagement, demos, procurement and contracts

## Shared engine workflow
1. **Classify:** detect business archetype(s), business model, buyer journey, geography, maturity, channel constraints and regulatory risk.
2. **Discover:** extract verifiable business facts, request consented connections and identify missing critical data.
3. **Diagnose:** map symptom to funnel stage, generate competing hypotheses and seek disconfirming evidence.
4. **Prioritize:** score expected impact range, confidence, effort, cost, speed, risk and operational capacity; make assumptions visible.
5. **Design:** produce at least three distinct interventions where justified: low-cost quick test, structural improvement and creative differentiated experiment.
6. **Approve:** request human approval for publication, ad spend, external messages, data exports and irreversible changes.
7. **Execute:** use available, permissioned platform connectors; otherwise produce exact ready-to-use artifacts and setup instructions.
8. **Measure:** define baseline, primary KPI, guardrail, attribution limits, minimum test duration and stopping criteria.
9. **Learn:** log hypothesis, segment, action, cost, observed outcome and confidence; update recommendations based on evidence, not generated prose.
