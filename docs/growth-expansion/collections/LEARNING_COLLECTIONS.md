# Operating the learning collections

Business-type playbooks, public cases, customer language, benchmarks, platform rules, and synthetic evaluation fixtures serve different purposes. Keep their evidence classes separate. A public company's retrospective does not become a tenant result. A simulated customer objection does not become an interview. A platform's capability description does not establish that the current tenant has permission to use it.

## From completed work to reusable learning

1. Preserve the original decision: diagnosis, alternatives, prediction, cost ceiling, measurement plan, and what would change the recommendation.
2. Record implementation separately: completed, partly completed, changed, not attempted, or unknown. Attach receipts or owner-provided evidence where available.
3. Record results only after the declared window, or mark them provisional. Include the population, unit, numerator, denominator, attribution method, and relevant costs.
4. Classify the learning: supported mechanism, unsupported mechanism, poor implementation, invalid measurement, insufficient evidence, or unresolved outcome. More than one classification can apply.
5. Ask what transfers. Identify prerequisites, counterexamples, and the strongest alternative explanation. Store the new hypothesis with reviewed confidence, not an automatic global truth.
6. Update affected playbook eligibility or scenario assumptions through review. Keep the old version and avoid transferring private observations across tenants without an appropriate privacy and rights design.

## Failure taxonomy

An implementation failure means the intended intervention did not occur correctly; it says little about its hypothetical effect. A measurement failure means the result cannot support the claimed decision. An economic failure can coexist with higher revenue. A customer-experience failure can coexist with higher conversion. An inconclusive test is a valid learning status and should not be relabeled a failed business idea to simplify the interface.

## Benchmark discipline

The companion benchmark capture file is an intentionally empty contract. No universal CAC, retention, click-through rate, conversion rate, or margin benchmark is assumed. Capture actual source population and metric definitions before comparing. A vendor's customer sample may be informative but unrepresentative; its median is not a target for every business. Prefer an owner's own consistent baseline when it is fit for the decision.

## Review criteria

Promote an observation into a reusable rule only when its scope is explicit, provenance can be inspected, the outcome is not merely a proxy substituted for success, and another reviewer can identify when the rule should not apply. Keep exceptional failures and dissenting cases visible. Learning quality improves when the system remembers its limits as clearly as its wins.
