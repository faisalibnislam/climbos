## Objection scope: resolution

Written follow-up: one evidenced answer per objection. Live call with an engaged buyer: distinct objections may be worked through in turn, one answer each. The same objection repeated, or any clear no, is final on both channels (BK09b-K3). Kazanjy's "loop until no objections remain" and "vague no hides a real objection" are not adopted (Book 11 §6: don't assume concealment).

## Conflicts with policy (policy wins)

- Default 60-day re-engagement with no date given (BK09b-K4). Rejected: re-contact only with permission.
- "Pricing may go up" / "slots are limited" as urgency (BK09b-K6). Rejected unless it is a confirmed fact (existing Never-do).
- Keep pressing for the meeting through objections (BK09a-F39). Rejected: one attempt in writing.
