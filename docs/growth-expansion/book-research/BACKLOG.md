# Runtime-code backlog from the book research

Edition 2026-10-20, against `growth-engine-v2` at `1321d8e`. **These are proposals; nothing here is implemented.** Each of the 77 book code gaps was checked against the repo at HEAD: 67 confirmed, 10 partly right, 0 wrong. They were merged into 46 items.

Order: correctness and safety first, then deterministic inputs and calculators, then prompt or skill text. Implementing an item means adding a unit test and running the existing suite. Items that change prompts or skills also need a bench comparison under an owner-approved cost cap.

## Greatest value

1. **CG-01, CG-03:** make "avoid if there's a leak" executable and add a stickiness gate. Paid scale and referrals stop feeding a leak.
2. **CG-02:** the SaaS diagnosis applies a no-card trial floor to freemium businesses, so normal freemium rates read as "weak". The citations in base-rates.md are also wrong.
3. **CG-04, CG-05, CG-21:** add a "test" play kind and channel test status, so Bullseye-style parallel tests aren't forced to fake profitability. Logged results should then move channel scoring.
4. **CG-06, CG-07, CG-08:** deterministic lints for unsupported competitor claims, fake urgency or reference prices, and step-1 cold-email links and "Re:" subjects.
5. **CG-09, CG-10:** add a sales-pipeline stage to diagnosis, fed from CRM deals and booking outcomes.

The owner's separate decision-code task (five decision books, 2026-10-20) covers diagnosis, eligibility, economics and outcome learning, and may implement or supersede some items here. Its report says which.
