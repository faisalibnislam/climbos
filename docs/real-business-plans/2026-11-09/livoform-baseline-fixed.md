## Where this could go

Become the form builder people pick when a form is part of how clients see them: branded, cheap per company, and buildable from inside AI assistants. A direction to test, not a revenue promise.

Who: Freelancers and small agencies who onboard new clients with intake, brief or quote forms and want each form under the client's name. This is a hypothesis from the site's use-cases page.

Why: Per-company flat pricing ($2 Core, published) with one company per client, own domain, no vendor branding and ready client templates. The free plan is the trial; assistant connections (Claude, ChatGPT) are a second door.

How it could build: Each agency that publishes client forms creates more client companies, more templates tested on real work and proof for the compare page. Respondents also see the brand on free-tier forms.
