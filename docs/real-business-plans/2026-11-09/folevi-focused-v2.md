## Where do my notes live?

Your notes live as files in a folder you pick: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or any other folder on your Mac. Your own cloud does the syncing, and our servers never see notes kept this way. If you ever leave, your pages are already files in that folder.

## What does a Folevi folder look like? A five-minute check

Each page you write becomes a file in the folder you chose, and you can confirm that in Finder in about five minutes.

1. Open Folevi, choose Keep notes on my devices, and pick a new folder called Notes inside iCloud Drive.
2. Create a page called Kyoto trip and type a few lines. Type / on a line to turn it into a checklist.
3. Open the Notes folder in Finder. Your Kyoto trip page should be there as a file.
4. Turn Wi-Fi off and add another line. Folevi keeps working, because your pages sit on your Mac.
5. Copy the Notes folder to a backup drive. The pages in it go with it.

That is what we mean by notes that stay yours: no export step stands between you and your pages. If you prefer an account later, you can move a library into it, or export it back into a folder.
