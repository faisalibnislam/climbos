Become the form tool small studios think of first for client-facing forms, built on useful free resources.

Who: Freelancers and small agencies who gather project details from clients. The people who teach them are a way to reach them.

Why: Publish free, stage-by-stage onboarding resources and show them to trusted teachers. Convert to paid only when users need their own domain, payments or AI replies, whose value for this audience is unknown.

## 14-day plan

Put Livoform's free client onboarding kit in front of people who teach freelancers and studios: one verified creator route, a second route we research, plus your own demo and post. Nothing goes out without your approval. In 14 days we learn whether outsiders reply, link or start the template. Paid interest can't be learned yet.

### Days 1–7: Prepare and send

Owner approves the drafts. ClimbOS finishes the checks and the second-route shortlist. After approval, each pitch is sent once and the owner's post goes live.

First action: Owner reads the three drafts and approves, edits or rejects each one.

How to check: Confirm Thomas Frank's current contact page, the Notion connection on Core, and that the demo shows only what the live template does. Confirm each extra route's own rules.

Next decision: Continue if checks pass. If a claim fails, change the copy. If a page now refuses pitches, drop that route and use the others plus the owner's post.

### Days 8–14: Observe and decide

Count replies, links and template starts per route. Decide the next cycle. Send no follow-up unless someone replied.

First action: Record the counts for each tagged link in one simple list.

How to check: Compare against the kit page's visits before sharing if Livoform's analytics have them. Otherwise treat these counts as the first baseline.

Next decision: Continue with the route that produced a reply, a link or template starts. Change the educator or angle if there are visits but no starts. Silence or sparse traffic is inconclusive: creators may reply after day 14, so carry measurement forward. Paid conversion waits for the 30–90 day window.

If it works: At 30 days, repeat with the best-fitting verified educators or communities, using what the first replies taught us. At 90 days, ask which template starts reached real client use, then study what Core and Pro add for paying users.
