## Where this could go

Become the trusted, coffee-priced home for small Mac utilities that fix everyday chores, where one $2 membership replaces hunting for one-off apps. This is a direction to test, not a revenue promise.

Who: A Mac user with one specific annoying chore (oversized photos or PDFs, a cluttered Downloads folder, a Mac that sleeps too soon) who would otherwise search for a single-purpose app.

Why: Task-first entry, then membership. People arrive through a chore (the Make-it-smaller collection), use a free app or a collection app, then see that the $2/month membership covers the other apps. Earned creator coverage supplies borrowed trust at no ad cost.

How it could build: Each new app and collection creates another chore-specific entry page. Each creator link and sign-up shows which chores convert, so the next page or app can be chosen with data.

## 14-day plan

Run one 14-day test at $0: rewrite the Make-it-smaller page around one chore, and pitch Mac creators for earned coverage. Learn tracked visits, free sign-ups and paying members. Demand at $2/month is unproven, so no revenue forecast is defensible.

### Days 1–7: Ship the page and first pitches

Publish the one-chore page with tracked links; send first 5 creator pitches including MacMost and Mark Ellis.

First action: Day 1: owner confirms free apps and approves the page block. Day 2: publish and test links. Days 3–5: send 5 approved pitches and make one post with a tracked link in a Mac community you already use.

How to check: By day 3, tracker shows short-link clicks and sign-ups; day 7 shows pitches sent and replies.

Next decision: If links do not record clicks by day 3, fix tracking first. Otherwise continue. Pause any creator who objects.

### Days 8–14: Read results, send second batch, decide

Send 5 more pitches, read the sign-up rate, and decide the next 14-day cycle.

First action: Day 8: owner approves second 5 pitches and one follow-up to non-repliers. Day 14: ClimbOS posts the results summary.

How to check: Day 14: sign-ups per 100 visits versus the prior 14 days; creator replies, coverage and paying members.

Next decision: Under 100 tracked visits: carry measurement forward. Continue if sign-ups rise and a member is tracked. Change the hook if flat. Stop if zero sign-ups at 100 visits. Zero replies at 20 pitches means rework.

## 90-day plan

Validate before spending: rebuild the Make-it-smaller page around one chore, and pitch Mac-workflow creators for earned coverage. $0 ad spend, about 3.5 owner hours a week (planned cap). Success means tracked paying members; demand at $2/month is unproven.

### Days 1–14: Launch the chore page and first pitches

Chore-first block live on the Make-it-smaller page with tracked links; first 10 creator pitches sent.

First action: Owner confirms the free-app list, approves the page block and publishes it with tracked links.

How to check: Weekly sheet: tracked visits, free sign-ups, paying members, pitches sent, replies. Compare with the prior 14 days.

Next decision: Continue if sign-ups per visit rise and a member appears. Change the hook if visits stay flat. Stop and rework the entry offer if zero sign-ups after 100 tracked visits. If under 100 visits, carry measurement forward.

### Days 15–30: Read the page result, finish the creator test

Decide on the page; send up to 10 more pitches; offer a creator-picked collection only to creators who reply.

First action: ClimbOS summarizes the 14-day page result; owner approves the second pitch batch.

How to check: Replies per pitch, coverage pieces, and members per creator link, against the 20-pitch limit.

Next decision: Continue creators if two replies and one coverage piece appear. Test an affiliate-style offer if they want payment. Stop creators if zero replies after 20 pitches.

### Days 31–90: Commit to what works, then review

If signals appear, build up to 2 more chore pages and a creator collection; otherwise rethink the entry offer. Day-90 review sets the next cycle.

First action: Pick the next chore page from the collection with the most tracked sign-ups.

How to check: Monthly sheet: members and sign-ups by route and chore page, owner hours used, comparison with cycle one.

Next decision: Continue routes with tracked paying members. Change or drop routes with visits but no sign-ups. No paid acquisition until margin and retention are known.

## Continue, change or stop

After 100 or more tracked page visits there are no free sign-ups, or after 20 pitches no creator replies. Then the task framing or the audience channel is wrong; rework the entry offer before more outreach.

If it works: If sign-ups and members appear from tracked links, build 2–3 more chore pages from the best-fit collections and a small creator-curated collection program. If not, rethink the entry offer before any acquisition.

Assumptions: Some Mac users search for chore-specific fixes (search demand is unknown; speculative).; A free app is the easiest first step into the membership (which apps are free is unconfirmed).; Mac creators will consider unsolicited indie apps when the demo fits their audience (unverified).
