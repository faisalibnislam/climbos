## Where this could go

Become known as the 'your notes stay your files' workspace for Mac, iPhone and iPad: docs, tasks and databases without an account or lock-in. This is a direction to test, not a revenue promise.

Who: Mac users on macOS 15 or later who juggle Apple Notes, Obsidian or Notion and are deciding where to keep notes, tasks and wikis.

Why: Use the free, no-account native apps as the entry point and make the paid step visible: Folevi Cloud (from $1.99/month, per its site) adds web, sharing and AI. Hooks pull the control lever (files in your own folder) and the ease lever (first note in five minutes). Reach people through an honest comparison page and earned Mac-tutorial coverage, not paid ads.

How it could build: Each honest comparison, tutorial mention and template adds a findable asset that sends Mac users to a free download. Files in the user's own folder may also reduce churn (hypothesis).
