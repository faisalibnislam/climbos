Who: A Mac user on a recent macOS (Diet Image lists macOS 26.0 or later) facing a concrete small task, such as a file too big to email or upload.

Why: Task-first entry pages on existing collections, with free apps as the no-cost first step and one $2 membership for every app on up to 6 Macs. Positioning: versus Setapp ($14.99/month for 1 Mac, 7-day trial; AI research notes, recheck) or a stack of free open-source apps, Macloving fits Mac users on recent macOS who want a few small utilities cheaply. Earned tutorials from Mac educators extend each page.

How it could build: Each task page, and each tutorial that links to it, stays live and can bring in new visitors. Per-source tracking shows which tasks and sources lead to paid starts.
