## Where this could go

Become known as the free, native Apple workspace where your notes stay your own files, with paid Cloud only when you want the web app, sharing, teams or AI. This is a positioning ambition, not a revenue promise.

Who: Individuals and small teams on Mac, iPhone and iPad who want docs, tasks and databases in one place but don't want an account, a lock-in or their notes on someone else's server.

Why: Use the free no-account apps as the entry point and Folevi Cloud ($1.99 to $13.99 a month, published) as the upgrade for web, sharing and AI. Distribute through the existing compare page and earned Mac-workflow creator coverage rather than paid ads.

How it could build: Tracked links show which source and message produce Cloud accounts. Each sourced compare page, template demo or creator mention then becomes a reusable asset for the next cycle.
