### Walkthrough: keep your notes as files in a folder you choose

Target: A help page on folevi.com (publish only after owner approval). Also usable as the link or attachment sent to MacMost if they say yes, and to any other publisher.. Unsent/unpublished draft.

Keep your notes as files in a folder you choose

Folevi's Mac app is free, with no account. Your pages are files in a folder you pick, so you own them.

Before you start: you need a Mac with macOS 15 or later.

1. Download Folevi for Mac from https://folevi.com/mac and drag it to Applications.
2. Open it and choose Keep notes on my devices. No sign-up is needed.
3. Pick a folder. iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or any folder works.
4. Write your first page. Type [[ to link to another page.
5. Open the folder in Finder. Your pages are there as files.
6. Turn off your internet and keep writing. Your edits stay on your device.
7. Press Option-Space in any app to add a task with Quick Add.

Good to know: sharing, the web app and AI credits need a free Folevi account. Paid Folevi Cloud plans start at $1.99 a month. You don't need any of that for the steps above.

If you move later, you can move a library to an account, or export it back into a folder.

Checks before use: Run every step on a Mac with macOS 15 or later and fix any that differ; Confirm the Quick Add shortcut is Option-Space in the current app; Add screenshots from your own app before publishing; Publish only after you approve it.
