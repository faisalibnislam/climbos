# Shared contact-frequency policy

17 November 2026. Follow-up to original requirements 118/141/146/148 and the owner's neutral-review and approval requirements. The master index routed this work to Book 08 sections 11–12. The corpus describes a desired shared policy; the runtime was inspected independently.

## Confirmed path and gap

`settings.setContactFrequency` already stored an owner-selected 1–90 day interval. `marketing.frequencyPreview`, `approveSend`, `claimSend` and `authorizeSendDispatch` checked the exact eligible broadcast audience, latest recorded broadcast/outreach sends and frozen recipients of earlier running or uncertain broadcasts. Reviews independently enforced suppression, active outreach, their 90-day invitation rule, daily limit and exact approved message/mailbox claims. Outreach independently checked duplicate sequences, queued reviews and mailbox scheduling constraints.

The shared interval did not cover review history in broadcasts, nor recent broadcasts/reviews consistently in outreach and review dispatch. Four new regressions first failed because sent, queued, sending and uncertain review invitations did not hold a broadcast.

## Implemented behavior

- `convex/lib/contactPressure.ts` now owns the existing bounded pending-broadcast recipient lookup, reused by all three workflows. Batch operations load it once within their transaction; they do not repeatedly load potentially large frozen recipient lists for every person. Incomplete or ambiguous pending audiences remain a hold.
- Review history is read for the exact tenant and normalized address. A new `reviewRequests.by_org_email_status_sent` index obtains the actual maximum recorded send time, regardless of row insertion order or how many older dated records exist. Queued, sending and uncertain invitations remain active until resolved.
- Undated sent receipts use an explicit recovery observation only as a conservative upper bound: a previously sent message existed by the observation time. This neither writes nor reports that time as `sentAt`. The owner sees a separate observation-based waiting explanation. Undated receipts without a usable observation and failed attempts without proof of non-send remain unknown. Known provider rejection and attributed owner-reported non-send release their hold without granting another send.
- Broadcasts join their existing encrypted contact address to review history only when the tenant has review records. Decryption retains tenant AAD and verifies the normalized address hash. Unreadable or mismatched identity stays unknown; plaintext addresses and cryptographic errors are not exposed in the frequency result. Existing contacts and reviews are reused; no plaintext contact field, parallel ledger or provider is added.
- Review preparation and approval exclude recipients held by the owner interval and explain the count. The same policy runs again in the transactional message claim, excluding the invitation itself. A new hold after approval prevents SMTP handoff and records a known unstarted failure through the existing path. A fresh owner preview and approval are still required later. Satisfaction, sentiment, ratings and optional private feedback have no part in eligibility.
- Outreach enrollment checks recent and unresolved marketing/review activity. Every dispatch claim, including a follow-up, rechecks the owner interval against recorded history. A known clearance time postpones that same approved step to the existing business send window, without consuming a provider attempt. Unresolved history pauses the campaign with `contact_limit`, keeps the step queued and requires owner review/resume. Existing first-touch duplicate checks remain intact; those checks can stop a conflicting first touch outright.
- Settings explain the policy's three covered workflows and the distinct 90-day review limit. The existing broadcast callout includes review holds and disables opening its Send confirmation while held. Review previews/results report excluded recipients. Outreach shows why a campaign was paused; existing attempt history retains the detailed reason.
- No interval is invented. Without an owner-set limit, existing behavior remains, including independent suppression, duplicate-flow checks and the review-specific interval. Essential service communications are outside this policy. No recommendation, preview or policy change grants external action authority.

## Remaining boundaries

This is coordination of the existing three email paths, not a complete lifecycle platform. Automatic customer-state ingestion, enrollment/exit checks, event-driven cadence adaptation and other lifecycle paths remain incomplete. Messages sent outside ClimbOS are not assumed known. Existing historical outreach/broadcast timestamp semantics are unchanged; this does not certify every legacy receipt as independently measured delivery.

Reads of undated review receipts, failed review attempts and duplicate contact records are capped at 100 per address; exceeding coverage holds the recipient as unknown. Pending broadcasts retain the existing eight-attempt / 10,000-recipient-per-attempt bounds. These are safety/read limits, not evidence of production throughput. A legacy sent/failed review with missing evidence, or a history exceeding these caps, can require a future migration/correction workflow; the current recovery UI does not resolve every legacy final status. It must not be silently treated as unsent to unblock a campaign.

Review holds can cause a smaller approved batch under the existing recipient-removal behavior; no new recipient or changed message is substituted. Outreach follow-ups can move later than their original cadence to honor a subsequently enabled owner interval. A clearance date is conditional on no newer contact or unresolved attempt. No production-scale load test, live mailbox compatibility check, full accessibility certification, hosted deployment or growth-impact validation was performed. The full 410-requirement and later-specification scope remains active and incomplete.
