# Exact booking and customer-record association — 17 November 2026

## Audit and confirmed gap

The existing booking flow creates or advances a deal and stores an optional prospect id, but did not retain the created deal id. Cancellation found direct-booking deals by name/time within 200 records, or the first prospect-linked deal. It could clear an owner-edited next step or downgrade a prospect with another confirmed call. Attendance notes reached only prospect-linked deals. A public booking reference could also advance a different prospect without matching the submitted address. These findings came from `outbound.book`, both guest and owner cancellation paths, CRM synchronization and the inbox outcome path; Book 11's buyer-progress guidance remains reference material rather than implementation evidence.

## Implemented behavior

- New bookings retain an exact tenant-scoped `dealId`. The booking-generated CRM next step separately records which booking owns it. Indexed tenant/deal/status/time and tenant/prospect/status/time reads locate the next confirmed appointment without guessing from a name.
- A prospect association requires a matching recorded address and either its matching reference or a unique address match. Forwarded/mismatched references and ambiguous CRM matches produce a separate direct-booking record, leaving unrelated records unchanged. The appointment remains bookable. This is an address-based association, not verified identity or email ownership; no verification email or new permission is inferred.
- Booking-generated next steps point to the earliest confirmed future appointment for that exact deal. Nonempty owner-entered steps are preserved. A meaningful owner change to the step, date, instant or stage clears booking ownership; simply saving the same value does not. A stored pointer alone is insufficient: the linked booking, tenant, expected “Call” text and instant must still agree.
- Cancelling the currently tracked appointment moves an untouched booking-generated step to the remaining exact linked call. Cancelling another appointment leaves the current step intact. If no call remains, the untouched booking-generated step is cleared and a booked deal can return to talking. A legacy upcoming prospect call prevents falsely declaring the booked stage over even when its exact deal relation is unavailable. Owner-edited steps and won/lost decisions remain intact. A new booking no longer reopens a lost prospect automatically.
- Attendance/correction notes reach the exact direct-booking deal even after its name changes, as well as valid prospect-linked deals. Cancelling and recording attendance use the same resolver. An explicit missing/foreign/conflicting link fails closed; it does not fall back to another record. Unambiguous legacy prospect links support recorded notes, but do not grant ownership of an older next step. Ambiguous historical links and name-only matches are not inferred or migrated.
- The inbox explains when an outcome is saved only on an unlinked booking. Owner outcome recording still creates no send, approval or scheduled no-show job. Existing guest/owner cancellation authorization and audit paths are retained. Other automatic prospect synchronization rejects duplicate deal associations rather than choosing the first one.

## Verification and remaining limits

The focused regression/permission run passed **62 tests across ten files**, with root/Convex typecheck and scoped lint. New cases cover two-call cancellation in both orders, same-value saves, owner edits, closed deals, direct-booking attendance after renaming, mismatched references, foreign explicit links, ambiguous legacy records and legacy upcoming calls. The public booking regression now supplies a recorded matching address; its former arbitrary ref-only association is intentionally rejected. An initial broader run exposed that outdated fixture expectation before it was corrected. See [Verification](../Verification.md) for commands and browser results.

Only isolated loopback fixtures were used; no real guest email, hosted deployment or paid model was used. Creation, reminders and cancellation notifications retain their existing behavior; this batch does not claim a complete review of their send/result authorization. Outcome recording remains separate from those notification paths.

There is no automatic entity merge, general customer identity verification, owner UI for relinking historical records, or retrospective reconstruction of ambiguous links. Separate direct bookings can still create separate deals. Existing legacy prospect associations are recorded context, not newly verified evidence. Manual/off-platform appointments remain unknown. Full retained stage history, personalized follow-up learning, and requirements 114/239 remain incomplete. No new provider, agent, calendar integration or strategy-engine version was introduced.
