# Saved customer follow-ups — 19 November 2026

## Confirmed gap and implemented path

The 18 November audit confirmed that the existing customer-progress workflow could filter one reviewed broadcast, but did not retain a journey, enrollment, elapsed waiting time, exit state or accepted-step history. The new increment extends that same contacts → owner progress → email draft → exact audience review → existing approval → existing provider receipt path. It does not add an agent, model, provider or alternative send executor.

Owners can use **Customers → Follow-ups** to save a name, contact segment, product/journey, reported state, exact definition, maximum observation age, and 1–5 existing email steps. Every step retains its exact artifact version and an explicitly supplied waiting time of 0–365 elapsed days. Saving remains a draft; starting tracking is a separate owner/admin action. No business cadence is guessed.

Active tracking checks complete, bounded contact/consent/progress data roughly every 15 minutes, continuing across batches of ten due journeys. A manual check is available. It enrolls recent matching opted-in contacts once. Missing/conflicting/stale reports hold existing entries; different progress/definition, segment departure, withdrawal, suppression or deletion exits them. Explicit progress corrections hold/exit existing entries immediately, including while paused. A periodic refresh can recover a held entry when matching evidence becomes usable. Completed/exited entries never automatically restart. New contacts begin their wait when actually enrolled, not when their record was created.

Each due step opens the existing email review with the saved email and audience rules locked. The UI exposes the matching count, excluded/unknown reports, waiting time, current state, last complete check and pause control. The ordinary per-send progress filter remains available. The existing planning context describes the capability without assuming that any journey is configured or enabled.

## Authorization and result boundaries

- Creation, start, refresh, pause, archive and scheduled eligibility checks have no send, provider, model, spend or live-price side effects. Start/resume explicitly assigns the responsible tracking owner while preserving the original creator.
- Existing email content approval, paid-plan entitlement, owner permission, provider account/list, exact recipient identities, consent/suppression, contact-frequency policy, quota reservation and dispatch checks remain authoritative. A suitable matching Resend list must be configured separately; the journey does not manufacture or silently modify one.
- Send approval additionally binds the journey id, revision and step, exact saved artifact/version and evidence for the enrolled contacts. Pause/resume increments the revision, so an old approval cannot become usable again after resuming. Changes after approval are rechecked before provider dispatch. A live pause from another owner window removes the send form.
- Only existing provider acceptance or verified recovery can record an accepted step. Uncertain/failed attempts, an unrelated email, content approval or task completion cannot advance it. Provider acceptance is not evidence of delivery, reading, activation, retention or revenue.
- Dispatch records the original enrollment identities as well as recipient hashes. A late receipt cannot advance a newly imported contact/new enrollment with the same address after erasure. Repeated callbacks/recovery do not advance twice. Late confirmed acceptance may record an already-dispatched step while a journey is paused; it does not grant another send or restart an exited contact.
- Contact deletion removes enrollment/accepted-step history. Workspace export includes bounded journey and enrollment records with explicit truncation; workspace purge includes both tables. Existing audit/receipt retention remains unchanged.

## Remaining limitations

- This is a bounded, owner-configured follow-up review workflow using **manual owner observations**, not a real-time product-event integration or unattended lifecycle email automation. Broader telemetry and complete cross-channel journeys remain partial.
- Current supported limits: 200 active group contacts and 200 retained enrollment records per journey; 5 steps; 20 saved journeys per workspace, including archived history. Exceeding a verification bound holds approval rather than using a sample. Draft choices are drawn from the latest 100 artifacts, with omissions disclosed; observation choices retain the existing 30-group limit. Export includes up to 20 journeys and 1,000 enrollment records, with a truncation flag.
- Saved rules/steps are immutable. Editing a selected email invalidates that saved step; use a new journey with the intended version. Pausing cannot recall a dispatched email. No per-customer local send time, automatically matching provider-list management, or arbitrary event trigger was added.
- Business cadence, customer progress, consent and current provider eligibility must be supplied/verified. Browser fixtures do not establish real deliverability, production operations, business usefulness or a fresh Livoform strategy-quality pass.
- Paid API calls remain paused; the recorded **$99.063401 used/held of $100** is unchanged. Runtime models, routes, budgets and quality thresholds are unchanged. No deployment, real campaign, PR or live offer change occurred. The unrelated owner `.gitignore` changes are preserved and excluded from the commit.
