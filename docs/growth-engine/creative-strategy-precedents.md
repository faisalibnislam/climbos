# Creative strategy precedents

Last reviewed: 25 October 2026. This is a curated retrieval set for strategy exploration, not a forecast database or universal playbook.

## Verified coverage

The current runtime library contains **33 editorially reviewed cases** (`CP-001`–`CP-033`). The requested target is 100 useful cases, leaving **67 cases unverified and not yet included**. The general growth case collection is separate and is not counted as creative-strategy coverage.

Each case records its reported choice and mechanism, evidence class, causal limits, transfer conditions and risks, a small-business adaptation, editorial audience/goal/constraint/scale/geography/business-model tags, and a reversible test. Runtime retrieval requires a mechanism match, then ranks with available audience, goal, constraint, scale, geography, and business-model context. The model receives short match explanations with each case rather than an opaque relevance score. Retrieval returns at most two cases to the production growth-plan task and never treats a precedent as evidence about the target business; when those context dimensions are absent, they do not contribute a match.

The sources cover Airbnb's election cereal boxes, Patagonia's "Don't Buy This Jacket" campaign, Nike's waffle-sole experiment, Mario's pixel-constrained character design, LEGO's fan-to-product review pathway, Amazon's decision to keep negative product reviews visible, REI's #OptOutside, Dollar Shave Club's timed founder-video launch, Warby Parker's at-home eyewear trial, Liquid Death's identity-led canned water, Spotify's personalized Wrapped ritual, IKEA's flat-pack distribution model and separate buyback/resale service, Duolingo/Netflix's entertainment-to-learning partnership, Burger King's location-triggered app offer, Michelin's customer travel guide, Domino's product change in response to customer criticism, Dropbox's collaboration-led bottom-up adoption, Nintendo Wii's shared accessible play strategy, Taco Bell/Frito-Lay's product-integrated brand partnership, Gmail's capacity-limited invitation beta, Ocean Spray's creator-centered response to a spontaneous social moment, Coca-Cola's personalized packaging invitation, KFC UK's accountable apology during a supply failure, Aravind's high-volume eye-care and cross-subsidy model, M-PESA's use and evolution of an existing airtime-dealer network, OXO's accessibility-led kitchen-tool redesign, Fresh Life's fee-for-service sanitation model, Hilti's managed tool-fleet service, Rolls-Royce's engine-hour-linked long-term service agreements, Blendtec's product-test video series, Old Spice's live character-led audience responses, and the Million Dollar Homepage's sale of finite pixel inventory. Outcomes retain their source status: company-reported counts, partner-reported implementation metrics, and retrospective before/after reports are not causal estimates. See `shared/knowledge/creativePrecedents.ts` for claim-level source records.

## Runtime use and editorial additions

Creative strategy is attached to the existing growth-plan task and its retrieved precedent context is injected into the growth-plays agent and critic, bounded to two cases per request. The owner can request a balanced, more conventional, more surprising, or more ambitious range; every request retains a bold option. The saved plan records the chosen range, exact task/input hashes, prompt contract version, and exact retrieved IDs, and keeps one grounded, one adjacent, and one bold option outside the ranked/costed execution plays. Each option records why a convention persists, what could be lost by breaking it, the specific action and customer behavior, transfer logic/differences or an explicit absence of precedent, separate ratings, and a reversible test with continue/change/stop evidence. Workspace editors can append decisions, owner-edited versions, test status, and evidence-labeled outcome notes; the next strategy run may use only that same workspace's records as untrusted preference/learning context. These notes do not establish causality. Proposals remain ideas; existing tenant, approval, destination, budget, and action-scope checks govern execution. The existing large-growth library remains a hypothesis source, not a verified case corpus.

Low-fit retrieval returns no precedent, and original bold ideas remain required even when retrieval is empty. Do not force an analogy based only on industry, invent cases to fill the 100-case target, reuse protected campaign expression, or copy reported outcome rates into a customer forecast. Add a case only after checking material claims against primary sources and, where useful, credible independent reporting. Record context, dates, evidence type, counterfactual limitations, transfer conditions, risks, and a reversible test; require editorial review before runtime use.
