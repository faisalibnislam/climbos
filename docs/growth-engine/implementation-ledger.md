## Resumption note (2026-10-24, engine and learning work)

When same-currency UTM billing and reported media spend overlap, channel context now also computes modeled contribution after media spend as net last-touch billing × valid owner gross margin − reported media spend. The value retains its own source field in the plan snapshot (v18) and is labeled as a non-causal proxy that may omit costs absent from the margin. It is not customer CAC or source-segment profitability; imported spend coverage, true new-customer identity, channel-specific margins and qualification-to-contribution joins remain unverified.

REQ-KNOW-01 now has three separately labeled agent-context layers: business truth (preserving source labels), business performance (preserving observational and attribution limits), and general guidance (role playbooks plus retrieved Growth Intelligence). A shared priority rule makes hard eligibility/safety limits binding, requires comparable performance evidence for outcome-based choices, and prevents one tenant's results or a generic book passage becoming a universal rule. The layers are prompt context over existing tenant-scoped records and approved library material, not new data stores or model training; no real-model or recommendation-outcome validation has been performed. Covered by `growth-knowledge.test.ts`.

The first 2026-10-24 strategy improvement now passes the computed business diagnosis into channel-fit research and final channel ranking. Only a `supported` diagnosis changes the shortlist: the existing channel journey-stage taxonomy brings channels relevant to demand, activation, conversion, retention, delivery or economics ahead of channels that only score well on generic priors. The score itself remains the base channel score; the scorecard labels diagnosis fit, and uncertain diagnoses preserve the old ordering. Deterministic unit coverage verifies the retention case and unchanged behavior without focus. This is still a stage-taxonomy proxy, not live-model or business-outcome validation.

The 2026-10-24 demand diagnosis no longer calls fewer than 1,000 monthly visitors a bottleneck. For an explicit customer goal, ClimbOS converts the required total/incremental customers into a monthly pace, including known churn for total goals; a total goal with a nonzero existing base and unknown churn stays unconverted. Goal pace, current customer gap, and a uniform-prior 90% range for visitor-to-customer conversion now appear for any business type when the owner supplied comparable funnel counts. Those numbers are explicitly a current-data scenario, not a forecast or root-cause claim. Only when SaaS lead-to-paid is not flagged weak does the diagnosis use the existing beta posterior policy to call reach/volume supported, and only at the configured 90% evidence threshold; otherwise the cause stays unknown. The signal does not prove that advertising, awareness, or any particular channel will fix the gap, and it has fixture coverage only.

The strategy run's fast first take now receives the same deterministic diagnosis and cross-business customer-goal pace as the full plan. Its prompt requires unknown/conflicting diagnoses to stay uncertain, supported diagnoses to be described as observations rather than proven causes, and a goal gap to remain separate from claims about advertising or channel impact. Diagnosis counts are explicitly owner-reported rather than independently confirmed; `factIndexes` may refer only to the ordered confirmed-facts list, which the output linker validates. This enriches the existing non-blocking first-take call without adding a model step; output quality has fixture coverage only and has not had a paid-model comparison.

Search Console now supports owner/admin discovery and exact selection of a property returned by Google's authorized property list, then stores tenant-scoped, dated query/page snapshots from the existing readonly integration. Daily sync keeps finalized data from a bounded 90-day window, records top-row truncation, and exposes the newest complete snapshot to channel-fit and SEO content jobs only when its property scope matches the recorded website. The prompts treat metrics as historical property/window observations, not universal demand or causal evidence; SEO refresh targets still require direct site inspection. The context is bounded and untrusted, mismatched properties are omitted, and the engine version is incremented. Mocked provider, property-scope, and tenant-isolation tests pass. **Gap:** no owner-facing property-selection/history UI, live Google credential or consent verification, persisted opportunity diagnosis, or measured recommendation/outcome evaluation; Google may omit low-volume queries and the bounded result is not complete market demand.

Issue 07 now has a structured owner-reported lifecycle stage (idea validation, pre-launch, early traction, growth, maturity, or possible decline). A missing stage stays unknown; website copy cannot set or infer it. Strategy context gives stage-specific planning guidance while labeling the stage as owner-reported, and the versioned plan snapshot captures it. A changed stage now marks the saved plan stale and blocks approval or starting a play; saving the same stage again does not. Tenant-scoped business editing and the onboarding read model expose the field for a later UI. This is a usable engine input, not an independent classification; frontend controls and stage-change outcome validation remain.

Issue 05 now asks research for structured competitor evidence across buyer, positioning, offer, pricing, proof, distribution, and experience. Each finding carries the exact page read and a separate cautious business implication; evidence URLs are rejected unless present in the current run's supplied sources. The optional records persist with business insights so later plans can use them. This makes comparisons more inspectable, but citations do not prove the page's claim, and the implication remains an inference rather than observed customer preference or competitive advantage.

Downstream social, ad, email, SEO and specialist content tasks now receive the saved competitor comparisons alongside customer language and positioning. Prompts distinguish competitor-published claims from independent evidence and prohibit copying, naming competitors, asserting superiority, or presenting their prices/features as current unless the owner specifically requests a sourced comparison. REQ-COMP-07 now has a scheduled refresh path: business name/site/type changes and owner edits to product, audience, pricing, offer, or competitor facts move research due time forward; otherwise the configurable default is 90 days (owner/admin may choose 0, 30, 60, 90, or 180 days). A daily bounded due queue starts the existing metered research job, respects credit availability and workspace approval thresholds, retries deferred work weekly, and skips unchanged businesses until due. A progressive cursor backfill initializes schedules for legacy businesses without scanning the full table in one run. This does not run against production here; scheduled live behavior and refresh quality have not been evaluated.

Channel recommendations now also receive the existing billing-based UTM source signal as a bounded source-by-source summary: payment-event count, identified customer count, first recorded payer count, net billing amount, and recorded refunds. Matching refunds follow their order; customer IDs are not exposed. The first-payer count uses the earliest positive payment in the loaded source-scoped ClimbOS billing history, so it distinguishes observed new payers from renewals while remaining incomplete if earlier history was never imported or a read cap is reached. An owner-facing import mutation accepts daily Google Ads and Meta Ads results in whole currency units, validates the period and amounts, replaces the same platform/date/currency row, and stores owner-reported provenance. When a recognized paid UTM source and its platform spend are available in the same currency and date window, ClimbOS computes tracked media ROAS and media spend per first recorded payer. If the owner provides a valid gross margin, a deterministic helper computes the media-only break-even ROAS used for a cautious screen; it excludes labor, fulfillment, tools, and other costs. The agent prompt and strategy snapshot preserve the window, partial-read state, and limits: UTM paths are descriptive last-touch associations, ROAS is not incremental lift or full ROI, the payer-cost proxy is not CAC, missing tags do not prove a channel had no impact, and none of these values alone establish profitability. Spend for other platforms, qualification-to-margin joins, variable delivery cost, and causal validation remain unavailable.

Issue 27 / Issue 127 now gets a deterministic, source-deduplicated trailing-365-day billing summary of gross payments, recorded refunds, net payments, and a same-window refund-amount share when that ratio is meaningful. Strategy agents receive it with explicit limits; it is also hashed into plan snapshot v2. If refunds exceed same-window gross (for example, because they relate to earlier purchases), or there is no gross denominator, no percentage is produced. The summary is not a customer refund probability, does not infer causes or refund reasons, and is marked incomplete when the shared 10,000-event read cap is reached. This makes refund exposure visible to recommendations but does not use it as a cohort forecast adjustment or separately itemize fulfillment costs.
