## Boundaries and remaining work

This is a connected decision-support feature, not a certified industry analysis or legal assessment. Source classes and indexes do not prove semantic support, causal effect, applicability of law, current source truth or market representativeness. The research budget cannot guarantee coverage of every force or external influence. Unassessed does not mean harmless. Full page excerpts, publication-date entailment, real model quality, diverse-business evaluation and actual business results remain unverified.

Pressure levels and directions are labeled model judgments, not measurements. No composite score ranks conventional ideas ahead of uncertain bold ideas. Grounded, adjacent and bold business models remain required. The owner must approve consequential work separately. A possible response does not change an offer, price, contract, supplier, ad budget, message or campaign. Browser fixtures and model mocks validate plumbing and boundaries; no paid model evaluation, hosted deployment or real customer action was performed. Targeted automated accessibility checks are not full conformance evidence.
