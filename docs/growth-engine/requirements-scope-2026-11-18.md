## Remaining work, separated by the evidence needed

1. **Fresh strategy acceptance — paused paid evaluation.** Saved outputs reveal failures, and local regressions prevent known mistakes. They cannot establish a fresh Livoform pass or consistent originality across industries. Reopen paid testing only on explicit owner authorization; never spend the remaining cap automatically. Human editorial review of saved material is possible without provider spend, but is not a new runtime evaluation.
2. **Business usefulness — actual owner/customer learning.** Tester access and founder time remain unknown. No customer experiments were run. Validate usefulness, operating effort, activation/retention and outcomes with consented evidence before claiming growth impact or expert equivalence.
3. **Current sources and platform setup.** Verify missing creative-case outcomes and volatile rules/fees against current primary sources; connect eligible owner accounts, scopes and approved destinations where relevant. These are setup/research constraints, not a reason to fabricate access or imply an API is supported.
4. **Broader product scope beyond current bounded paths.** Full lifecycle telemetry/enrollment, sales-call recording/transcription, continuous cross-engine visibility, arbitrary external-site editing/re-rendering and exhaustive customer journeys remain unavailable or partial. They require separately specified integration/engineering and acceptance work; the shared specialists and libraries are not substitutes for them. This audit does not mark those requirements complete.
5. **Release acceptance.** Representative-owner usability, full-device/accessibility review, production performance, operational recovery and authorized live-provider smoke tests remain. The isolated demo verifies application behavior, not live deliverability, external publication, provider eligibility or production operation.

The 22 historical roadmap items were a limited engineering batch, not a complete requirement checklist. The 410 requirements remain partially accepted at their broad scope; the linked inventory preserves each boundary instead of declaring wholesale completion from a green test suite.
