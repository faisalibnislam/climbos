# ClimbOS capabilities and boundaries

Failed plans now show their saved reason on the main plan, with further reasons and fixes expandable. Explain these checks prepares an editable assistant request for the saved plan; it preserves any existing unsent question and does not submit it or authorize work. Older plans without a saved explanation say so explicitly. This improves access to the recorded checks, not the correctness of every AI explanation or verified owner comprehension. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Starter and focused plans now apply the existing demand-validation policy to recommended channel spend. When supplied evidence calls for validating demand, a primary paid-acquisition allocation fails plan checks and cannot be approved or started. Channel priority, including ties, determines the primary choice. Supporting paid tests remain available for review, and grounded/adjacent/bold exploration stays visible. This is a check on explicit proposed spend and supplied concerns, not proof of demand or a detector for every paid tactic. Historical plans are not automatically revalidated. See [Verification](Verification.md).

New real v2 business-model checks are validated again before becoming saved recommendations. Invalid citations, impossible dates, contradictory unknown-price fields and malformed alternative portfolios cannot be accepted solely because an AI reviewer passed them. Source checks preserve distinct paths and query values. A failed attempt is shown separately from any earlier valid check. Bold alternatives still need neither a matching precedent nor high confidence. These are structural and evidence-reference checks, not proof of business viability or pivot effectiveness. See [Verification](Verification.md) and [the scoped audit](growth-engine/business-model-completion-audit.md).

Trend checks distinguish a bounded set of explicit generic exclusions, such as **“don’t chase viral trends”** or **“no viral gimmicks,”** from references that still need evidence. A separate current-trend reference elsewhere in the post remains subject to validation, and negative wording never waives checks on supplied trend metadata. Plural references such as “follow current trends” are now detected too. This is a deterministic English-language check, not complete understanding of intent. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Social trend checks now cover carousel text, video scripts, shot directions, visual briefs, image ideas, alt text, first comments and hashtags as well as captions, hooks and angles. Weekly planning also checks trend references in proof and calls to action. New social generation rechecks the kept output against its actual research record before images or draft saving; unresolved evidence errors stop that run even when the AI reviewer approves it. Evergreen creative ideas remain available without trend evidence. Existing voice/humor preferences and owner-approved examples were verified at both individual-post and weekly-plan generation boundaries. These are bounded evidence-link checks, not continuous trend monitoring or proof of audience fit. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Home’s **Review prepared work** opens the exact saved step result in the existing private draft editor. Newer attempts do not silently replace an open draft, and edits stay with the selected version. Home reads the latest attempt for each step in the selected plan; unrelated history cannot hide it, and a failed, running or malformed latest attempt does not revive an earlier success for that step. Viewers can read but cannot prepare or save work. Opening the review does not run AI, complete a task or authorize sharing. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Fresh demo workspaces and **Update my plan** requests now use the simple/starter plan format: a focused recommendation, prepared private brief, three strategy options and a matching recordable experiment. Initial examples include the next-step timeline and start the experiment as proposed. The adapter uses the complete current schema and existing save/review workflow. Its content is explicitly synthetic, with unknown business outcomes, capacity and baselines; it claims neither research nor an AI quality review. Existing demos and private edits are preserved when reopened; an update remains a separate version awaiting approval. Explicitly detailed demo requests retain their earlier fixture format. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Saved grounded, adjacent and bold options are now readable in assistant discussions through the existing plan tool. The tool keeps the three modes and their separate judgment dimensions, provides the original idea/evidence/test sections, and exposes only version-matched owner feedback with retirement visible. Low evidence, feasibility or lack of a matching precedent does not filter a bold option. Reading or discussing does not select an option or authorize execution. This exposes saved reasoning; it does not establish the model's originality or the idea's effectiveness. See [Verification](Verification.md).

After recording a primary test result, owners can choose **Discuss next step** to fill an editable assistant request. Nothing is submitted automatically. The existing assistant plan tool can now read an exact saved plan and its latest experiment reports, including original test conditions, recorded measurements, comparison unknowns, source labels and changed-definition limits. Oversized details are omitted as whole records with a way to request one test separately. Reading a result grants no action authority, and these records do not establish causal impact or the quality of the assistant's advice. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Focused plans now show **Check your progress** directly in Next steps: the saved primary test's metric and proposed observation window, expandable audience/comparison/stop rules, and the existing outcome form and history. Reporting does not require opening Research. Missing or duplicate test links remain explicit instead of selecting an unrelated test; discussing a missing test fills the existing editable assistant draft. Viewers can read saved results but cannot submit reports. The selected plan's tests no longer disappear behind newer plans' tests in the workspace read limit. This improves access to recorded learning, not independent outcome verification or automatic execution. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Experiment reports now support the actual proposed measurement: a count, time or another continuous value, or a monetary amount with its recorded currency. Owners record the unit, population/calculation scope and optional comparison; blanks stay unknown and zero stays zero. Money preserves currency precision and permits reported negative contribution. Corrections retain earlier values; changing units, currency or scope creates a separate analysis. The same reported values and limits reach later strategy context. These aggregate observations do not establish a conversion rate, significance, causal lift or an automatic success verdict. See [Verification](Verification.md).

New focused and starter plans include a proposed primary learning test in the existing experiment workflow. Owners can record met, missed, unclear or not-run outcomes, and the existing results reader carries those reports into later planning. Unknown baseline and sample inputs remain unknown; a short private observation need not pretend to be a powered A/B test. One-day observational windows are preserved. The learning record must match the primary move by name, include its audience, unit, metric, comparison, window and stop rule, and retain recorded evidence classes. This structural match does not prove that the test measures the right mechanism or that a reported outcome is causal. Creating a recommendation or recording a result grants no execution authority. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Experiment learning now carries the original focused recommendation alongside its reported tests, including saved offer economics, eligibility, capacity, timing evidence and unknowns. Each original plan appears once; newer plan terms do not replace it. Large decisions are omitted intact with a visible limitation rather than cutting away caveats. These are historical proposals, not proof that the experiment used those terms, reached that audience or ran in that window. Broken original business/goal/run links are excluded. The next strategy writer and reviewer receive the context; results still grant no action authority. See [Verification](Verification.md).

These are bounded validation checks, not proof of event relevance, source truth or live-model judgment. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Focused and starter recommendations now retain the existing structured offer assessment when the primary move proposes a promotion. Empty payment/delivery cost categories no longer imply zero costs. After plan approval, the focused offer’s terms enter the existing downstream drafting constraints; prepared drafts can distinguish a new assessed proposal from earlier approved offers. This never applies a discount or changes a live price. Exploratory grounded/adjacent/bold ideas remain separate from this execution assessment. The checks do not establish source truth, causal effects or live-model decision quality. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Prepared public drafts now reuse the selected approved offer’s price, mechanism, eligibility and urgency checks. When multiple approved offers exist, a draft must name its exact approved play. Validation-only promotions stay internal test briefs. Recognized unsupported money-back/refund promises and approved-offer mismatches block plan approval even when the AI reviewer approves the writing. Checks are bounded language rules, not complete semantic or legal verification. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Focused and starter plans now receive and retain the same deterministic owner-data diagnosis as detailed plans. The writer and reviewer see supported weak links, competing explanations, missing inputs, conflicts and capacity limits. Research → plan details → Where you stand exposes an expandable reading of the reported numbers, separate from AI prose. The diagnosis informs the immediate recommendation without removing grounded, adjacent or bold exploration. The active Add a few numbers form now preserves the saved funnel period, qualified-lead count/rule, activation meaning and signup type. Saving only economics leaves the funnel record unchanged. Owners can inspect and edit these definitions with the counts; blanks remain unknown and setup is distinguishable from receiving value. This repairs input integrity, not independent measurement or causal diagnosis. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Replanning can now reuse recent channel-fit work when the business evidence is unchanged, even after ClimbOS creates another untested plan. Empty proposal histories no longer masquerade as new observations in channel scoring; they remain in the strategy writer's full context with outcomes marked unknown. Reported results, invalid-evidence warnings, experiment outcomes and owner feedback remain available to the scorer. This reduces redundant scoring in the verified cases; it does not establish a production latency or cost saving. See [Verification](Verification.md).

Published social posts now have a Post results panel for member-reported qualified visits, signups, customer outcomes, operating time and separate engagement counts. It uses each post once with its current version text, source, method, dates and unknowns. Invalid or legacy observations cannot silently fall back to an older report. Learning considers the newest 120 report rows first, then up to 120 past slots from 28 days, preserving whole records within its context limit. Corrections consume rows in the read limit but count once per post in context. Omitted records are disclosed. ## Availability labels

- **Built:** implemented in the application. Test evidence is reported separately in [Verification](Verification.md); this label alone does not mean every current check has passed.
- **Needs setup:** the code exists, but an owner connection, operator credential, provider review, or workspace permission is required for real use.
- **Limited:** available only for a bounded sample or specific workflow, as described.
- **Unavailable:** no supported integration or behavior is implemented; use the stated manual or export path.

The Connections screen reports the current state for each workspace. Credentials and provider approvals can change availability without a code change. Demo workspaces use labeled fixtures; a fixture result is not evidence of a live provider operation.

## Boundaries

- Business diagnosis supports same-day buying. When timing comes from a broad buying-cycle category, the plan identifies the assumed duration and whether the category was owner-selected or inferred. Low-confidence inferred categories are not used as timing inputs. Short observation periods prompt a check of actual lead and purchase dates; channel timing scenarios do not predict when revenue will arrive.

- New offer and campaign-timing recommendations check evidence labels against recorded source types. Owner reports stay owner-reported even when they mention analytics; a web claim remains source-reported. Mismatched labels prevent approval. Reported risks are shown as reported by your team or a source. These checks do not independently verify the claim, and older saved plans are not reclassified.

- Landing drafts linked to a website audit can be captured on desktop and mobile, showing the exact saved version. Captures are labelled as the ClimbOS-hosted draft and never published. Choosing a design direction keeps the original page's text and screenshots past the 30-day screenshot limit.
- Design measurement plans need audience, guardrail, observation period and decision rule before publishing. Each finding keeps a review history.
- The daily, weekly, and monthly calendar shows review drafts, scheduled posts, and Autopilot slots. Each source loads in pages with an explicit Load more control and partial-count wording. Review drafts are date-filtered before pagination; newer work outside the range cannot hide older drafts. Unlinked slots open their exact slot; linked posts open the content review. The compatibility snapshot endpoint remains capped at 300 rows per source for older clients; the current calendar uses the paginated endpoint.
