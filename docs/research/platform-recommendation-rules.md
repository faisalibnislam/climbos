# Platform recommendation rules

Reviewed 2026-10-25. This is a source register for recommendations, not authorization to access or publish to any platform. Recheck official rules, eligibility, commercial terms, API permissions, and retention before enabling an integration or action. When not verified, offer a manual link or owner-entered evidence and label the limitation.

## Keep platform jobs separate

- **Review and comparison:** Google Business Profile, G2, Trustpilot, relevant software/category directories, and eligible local directories. Intended job: reputation, local discovery, or buyer comparison. Measure qualified profile visits, enquiries, signups, activation, retention, and owner time where available. Reviews must remain authentic and invitations neutral.
- **Launch community:** Product Hunt. Intended job: launch readiness, early-user feedback, and qualified referral discovery. Measure qualified visits, signups, activation, retention, feedback quality, and operating time. Votes and launch-day position are not customers or revenue.
- **Owned content and discussion:** the business blog, Reddit, Medium, niche forums, newsletters, creator and industry platforms. The intended job must be named per platform: customer research, useful participation, search discovery, referral, launch feedback, or authority. Measure qualified outcomes and questions answered; impressions, comments, or votes alone do not show customer value.

Only recommend a specific platform when the profile contains evidence for customer relevance, buyer intent, geographic/category eligibility, and the team's capacity and budget. Show which evidence is owner-reported, measured, or source-reported; include its source and date, confidence, unknowns, setup/maintenance effort, and a useful measurement plan. If buyer usage is unknown, ask the smallest useful question or suggest a manual check. Do not turn that unknown into a positive recommendation or forecast.

## Review invitation contract

The public invitation copy and destination are the same for every selected customer. Private feedback is optional and equally visible; it never changes the public invitation. No satisfaction, sentiment, rating, or feedback filters; no incentives; no generated or posted reviews on a customer's behalf. Before sending, preview and bind tenant, exact template version/content hash, recipient scope, review destination, and mailbox; require explicit owner approval and an audit record. Recheck unsubscribe/suppression and current destination before each send.

External action is not enabled by this source register. Any future platform sync/publishing change needs a fresh official-doc review, scoped credentials, owner approval bound to the exact content/action/destination, tenant authorization, and regression tests.
