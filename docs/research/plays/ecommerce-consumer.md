# Growth play library: ecommerce, DTC, consumer apps, creators and makers

Status: research, 17 Oct 2026. Scope: non-obvious, evidence-backed growth plays for small teams in four segments: DTC and ecommerce brands (food, apparel, home goods, beauty), B2C subscription apps (habit, health, utility), creators and course or coaching businesses, and makers on Etsy or Shopify. Budgets are assumed to be under $5k/month and teams under 10 people.

These are plays the ClimbOS engine can propose, staff with drafts and research, and track. Social posting and email newsletters are covered elsewhere and appear here only when they are a component of a larger play.

## How to read this file

**Ordering.** Plays are ranked by *predictability × leverage*:

- **Predictability.** How tightly inputs map to outputs once the play is running, and how much the owner controls the inputs. Volume-based plays, like 300 creator pitches a month, sit at the top. Plays that depend on a single editor's or Apple's decision sit lower.
- **Leverage.** Upside per hour and dollar for a small team, including compounding effects such as content you can reuse as ads, links, or reviews that keep converting.

**Evidence grade** (shown in each heading):

- **strong**: primary data, a large-sample study or platform documentation.
- **moderate**: several independent practitioner or vendor sources that agree.
- **weak**: a single vendor, anecdotes, or sources that conflict.

Almost all channel benchmarks in this field come from vendors who sell the channel. Those numbers are labelled, and the ranges given are deliberately conservative. A number marked "practitioner estimate" is my synthesis, not a cited figure.

**Research limits.** This pass ran out of web-search budget before covering paid sampling networks (Sampler, subscription-box placements), Amazon Creator Connections, journalist-request platforms (Qwoted, Featured) and Etsy-specific ads. Those plays are folded into neighbouring plays or listed under "Gaps" at the end, so they are not missing silently.

**Compliance baseline that applies to every creator, affiliate and review play:**

- FTC Endorsement Guides (revised June 2023). A free product counts as a material connection and must be disclosed clearly. Advertisers can be liable for unsubstantiated claims their endorsers make ([Hogan Lovells summary](https://hoganlovells.com/en/publications/ftc-publishes-updated-endorsement-guides-and-proposed-rule-banning-fake-reviews), [FTC Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)).
- FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials (announced 14 Aug 2024). It bans incentives *conditioned on sentiment*, review suppression, undisclosed insider reviews and buying fake followers or views. The FTC can seek civil penalties against knowing violators ([FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)).

ClimbOS should enforce both rules in every brief and outreach template it generates.

## 1. Opt-in creator seeding funnel [evidence: moderate]

- id: creator-seeding-optin-funnel
- fits: DTC brands and makers with a product that photographs or demos well, landed COGS under about $25 and gross margin of at least 55%. You need at least 50 units a month for gifting. Works for food, beauty, apparel, home goods and pet. Not suitable for regulated health claims unless creators are briefed on exact claim language.
- why it works: Gifting buys a *chance* at content at a fraction of a paid post's cost, so it is a numbers game with stable ratios. An opt-in step ("want one?" before shipping) removes most of the waste. That waste is why marketers broadly report weak returns from untargeted gifting: in one late-2025 analysis, 94% send product but only 19% see meaningful advocacy.
- steps:
  1. Define 3 creator personas by audience, not follower count (e.g. "home cooks in Southeast Asian cuisine, 3k–50k followers, US"). Exclude creators whose last 10 posts include more than 3 sponsored ones.
  2. Build a list of 300–500 matching creators a month from TikTok and Instagram search, hashtag co-occurrence and the "who else did this creator tag" graph. The free LTK Brand Platform (opened at no cost in Sept 2025) adds discovery, DMs and gifting tools.
  3. Send a short opt-in pitch, with no product shipped yet. It covers who we are, why them specifically (reference one post), what we would send, "no posting obligation", and a link to a 3-field form for size or variant and address.
  4. Ship within 48 hours of opt-in. Include a handwritten-style note, a unique discount code and a one-page "what makes this different" card with 2–3 approved claims.
  5. Follow up on day 7 ("did it arrive / any questions"), then on day 21. If they post, ask for usage rights on the post (paid amplification option) and invite them into the affiliate program (play 17).
  6. Track the funnel for each wave: pitched → opted in → shipped → posted → code redemptions → creators re-engaged.
  7. Every month, kill the persona with the worst post rate and double the best one.
- cost & effort: Product plus shipping, often $10–$35 per creator all-in. 300 pitches a month is about 6–10 owner hours if ClimbOS drafts and the owner approves. No platform fees are required.
- time to first result: First posts in 10–21 days. A readable funnel after 2 monthly waves.
- expected results:
  - Response to outreach: 3%–35% depending mostly on personalisation ([Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/influencer-outreach-email-templates)).
  - One seeding-software vendor's benchmark: about 30% response, 80% of responders shipped, and 30–50% of shipped creators producing content ([Saral](https://help.getsaral.com/en/articles/188-how-do-i-read-and-understand-the-seeding-funnel-data)).
  - Planning range for an opt-in funnel: 300 pitches → 45–90 opt-ins → 15–40 posts per month (practitioner estimate derived from the above). For untargeted gifting with no opt-in, assume a post rate near 19–30% ([HireInfluence](https://hireinfluence.com/blog/influencer-product-seeding-a-brands-guide/), [Elev8or gifting guide](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/influencer-gifting-and-product-seeding-guide)).
  - Sales from posts are highly skewed. Expect 1–3 creators out of 30 to drive most code redemptions (practitioner estimate). Their content is the real asset for ads (play 5).
- leading indicators: Opt-in rate (should be at least 15% of pitched). Ship-to-post within 21 days. Saves and shares per post. Code redemptions in the first 72 hours after a post.
- risks & compliance: The creator must disclose the gift (e.g. "#gifted" or "#ad") under the FTC Endorsement Guides even with no posting obligation ([FTC Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)). The brand is liable for claims creators make, so ship the approved-claims card. Never condition the gift on a positive review (FTC 2024 rule). Shipping to creators abroad raises customs and food-import issues.
- ClimbOS can automate:
  - ClimbOS: build the persona-matched creator list with contact routes (bio email, link-in-bio, DM handle), score each creator for audience fit and sponsorship saturation, draft personalised opt-in pitches that reference a real post, host the opt-in form, generate the claims card and note, track the funnel per wave, and draft follow-ups and rights-request messages.
  - Owner: approve the list and pitches, pack and ship (or connect a 3PL), and send DMs where email is unavailable.

## 2. TikTok Shop sample-to-content machine [evidence: moderate]

- id: tiktok-shop-sample-machine
- fits: DTC brands in the US or UK with AOV under about $60, a demo-able product (beauty, food and snacks, home gadgets, apparel basics, pet), at least 60% gross margin and the ability to fulfil TikTok Shop orders within its SLA. Poor fit for high-consideration or regulated products.
- why it works: TikTok Shop's affiliate marketplace lets thousands of commission-hungry creators request free samples. Each sample turns into several videos, and those videos carry a buy button. At small scale, creator videos already drive about half of shop GMV. The ratio of samples to content to GMV is stable enough to plan against.
- steps:
  1. Open TikTok Shop, list 1–3 hero SKUs, and set an open-plan commission of 15–20% plus a higher "targeted collaboration" rate of 20–30% for invited creators.
  2. Set sample-approval criteria: at least 1k followers, recent GMV in the category, a posting cadence of at least 3 a week, and no history of unfulfilled samples. Do not approve everyone, because indiscriminate approval is a common failure mode.
  3. Send about 30 samples a month at the smallest tier and ramp toward 200 a month as GMV grows.
  4. Give every sampled creator a 1-page brief: 3 hooks that worked, the product's single "wow" moment, banned claims, and a post deadline of 7–14 days.
  5. Monitor which videos convert. Spark-boost the top 5% with ad spend (see play 5) and offer those creators a retainer or a higher commission.
  6. Re-sample the creators who produced and drop those who didn't.
  7. Review unit economics weekly, because all-in platform costs can reach 35–45% before COGS.
- cost & effort: Samples (COGS plus shipping), commission of 15–30% on attributed sales and platform fees. About 5 owner hours a week for approvals and creator messages.
- time to first result: 2–4 weeks to first affiliate videos. 2–3 months to a stable sample-to-GMV ratio.
- expected results:
  - At $0–$12.5k monthly GMV, a vendor dataset shows about 30 samples/month → about 180 creator videos/month (about 6 per sample), with creator videos driving 51% of GMV. At about $100k/month, it shows about 400 samples/month and 73% of GMV from creator videos ([AmpliSell](https://amplisell.com/blog-posts/30-samples-won-t-build-a-tiktok-shop-channel)).
  - A program with creators and samples flowing but GMV under $1k/month is a common failure state, usually caused by over-approval or a weak hook ([AmpliSell](https://amplisell.com/blog-posts/why-your-tiktok-affiliate-program-isn-t-working)).
  - Small sellers (under $15M revenue) grew TikTok Shop sales 66% in 2025 ([Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/tiktok-shop-smb-growth-2025-implications-2026)).
  - No verified average ROI exists, and widely repeated stats lack methodology ([Syncly](https://syncly.app/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-roi-in-what-the-data-actually-shows)).
- leading indicators: Sample-to-video rate within 14 days, videos per sample, the percentage of videos with at least one order, and GMV per sample.
- risks & compliance: All-in fees and commissions of 35–45% can erase margin ([FiveX](https://fivex.com/blog/tiktok-shop-creator-economics/)). Fulfilment SLA penalties apply. Creators must use TikTok's commercial content toggle, and FTC disclosure still applies. Health and beauty claims are moderated. Sample abuse (creators who never post) is common, so keep approval criteria strict.
- ClimbOS can automate:
  - ClimbOS: shortlist creators by category GMV, draft invitations and briefs, write hook scripts, track which sampled creators posted, flag non-posters, monitor per-video conversion and generate the weekly unit-economics view.
  - Owner: set up and verify the shop, approve samples, ship or connect a 3PL, and fund Spark boosts.

## 3. Post-delivery review and photo-review engine [evidence: moderate]

- id: review-photo-engine
- fits: Any DTC brand or maker with at least 30 orders a month, and apps (see play 13 for in-app). Highest leverage for products with fewer than 5 reviews per SKU, and for higher-priced items.
- why it works: The jump from zero to about 5 reviews is where most of the conversion effect lives. Spiegel Research Center found that products with 5+ reviews are up to 270% more likely to be purchased than those with none, and the lift was larger for higher-priced items (up to 380% vs 190%). The effect flattens after about 5 reviews on high-priced products. A request timed to delivery, not shipment, collects them mechanically.
- steps:
  1. Pull SKUs into three buckets: 0 reviews, 1–4 and 5+. Prioritise the 0 bucket for the best-sellers.
  2. Trigger the request on carrier "delivered" plus N days (N = time to first use: 3 days for food, 10 for skincare, 14 for apparel after the return window).
  3. Make it a one-tap star rating in the email body, then ask for an optional photo.
  4. Offer the *same* incentive for any honest review, e.g. entry into a monthly draw or points, and never tie it to stars.
  5. Send one reminder at +5 days. Do not send a third.
  6. Route 1–3 star reviews to a same-day personal reply from the owner, and publish the fix.
  7. Syndicate photo reviews to product pages, Google (via Merchant Center product reviews feed) and ads.
- cost & effort: A review app at $0–$60/month. Setup takes 3–4 hours, then it runs itself.
- time to first result: 2–4 weeks.
- expected results:
  - Review request response rates for post-delivery email typically sit at 5–15% of orders (practitioner estimate). Plan on 5 reviews per SKU taking about 50–100 orders of that SKU.
  - Conversion effect: up to 270% higher purchase likelihood at 5+ reviews versus none ([Spiegel/Lipscore summary](https://lipscore.com/blog/product-reviews-increase-your-online-sales-by-270/), [Spiegel](https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/from-reviews-to-revenue/)).
  - The only *causally identified* estimates are much smaller, at roughly 5–9% revenue per star of rating ([Cleancommit critique](https://cleancommit.io/blog/do-product-reviews-increase-conversion-rate/)). Plan on the conservative number, and treat 270% as the 0→5 ceiling.
- leading indicators: Review request open and submit rate, photo attach rate, the percentage of revenue from SKUs with 5+ reviews, and product-page conversion by review bucket.
- risks & compliance: The FTC 2024 rule bans incentives conditioned on positive sentiment, review suppression and undisclosed insider reviews, with civil penalties ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)). Amazon prohibits incentivised reviews entirely, so use Vine (play 22). Do not filter out negative reviews.
- ClimbOS can automate:
  - ClimbOS: bucket SKUs, write the request and reminder sequences, draft owner replies to negative reviews, cluster review text into product-page FAQ and ad hooks, and pick photo reviews for ads.
  - Owner: choose the review app, approve replies, and fix the product issues that reviews surface.
- sources:
  - https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/from-reviews-to-revenue/
  - https://lipscore.com/blog/product-reviews-increase-your-online-sales-by-270/
  - https://cleancommit.io/blog/do-product-reviews-increase-conversion-rate/
  - https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials

## 4. Post-purchase referral with cash or commission reward [evidence: moderate]

- id: post-purchase-referral
- fits: DTC brands with repeat or giftable products and at least 100 orders/month. Subscription boxes. Makers with strong unboxing. Needs a margin that can fund a $10–$20 reward or a 10–15% commission.
- why it works: Most happy customers say they would refer but few do: 83% say they would and 29% actually do. The fix is prompting at peak satisfaction and paying a reward the referrer values. A 2025–26 benchmark of 500 Shopify stores found cash or commission rewards outperform coupons by 2.7–4.5×.
- steps:
  1. Choose a reward. Default to a double-sided offer: the friend gets 15% off, and the referrer gets $10–$20 store credit *or* cash/commission. Test cash against coupons.
  2. Place the prompt in 4 spots: the thank-you page, the shipping-confirmation email, the post-delivery email (with play 3) and inside the package as a QR card.
  3. Give each customer a personal link and a short vanity code they can say aloud.
  4. Pre-write share messages for SMS, WhatsApp and Instagram DM, not just social posting.
  5. Send a monthly "you've earned X" reminder to customers with unused links.
  6. Review the share rate and referred conversion after 90 days, then change only one variable at a time.
- cost & effort: A referral app at $0–$200/month plus rewards. About 4 hours to set up.
- time to first result: First referred order in about 14 days on median. 68% of programs see one within a month and 89% within 90 days ([ReferralCandy 2025–26 benchmark](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/referral-program-benchmarks-whats-a-good-conversion-rate-in-2025)).
- expected results:
  - Share-click rate (share clicks ÷ prompt views): top-quarter programs reach 4.64% and the top 10% reach 13.38% ([ReferralCandy](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/referral-program-benchmarks-whats-a-good-conversion-rate-in-2025)).
  - Referred-visitor conversion: about 3–5% median per a separate vendor page ([ReferralCandy referral rates](https://referralcandy.com/blog/referral-rates)).
  - Referral share of revenue: 5–10% for mature DTC programs ([Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-referral-revenue-share-by-vertical-2026)). Plan on 2–6% in year one (practitioner estimate).
- leading indicators: Prompt view → share-click rate, shares per sharer, referred-visit → order rate and reward redemption.
- risks & compliance: Cash rewards may need tax reporting above thresholds, and you should check your payout provider. Referral spam to strangers hurts deliverability, so restrict sharing to personal channels. A referral reward is a material connection: referrers posting publicly must disclose it.
- ClimbOS can automate:
  - ClimbOS: write all prompt copy and share messages, design the insert card, run the reward A/B test plan and report funnel metrics monthly.
  - Owner: pick the app and the reward budget, and approve.
- sources:
  - https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/referral-program-benchmarks-whats-a-good-conversion-rate-in-2025
  - https://referralcandy.com/blog/referral-rates
  - https://www.rivo.io/blog/referral-program-statistics
  - https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-referral-revenue-share-by-vertical-2026

## 5. UGC ad library plus creator-handle ads [evidence: moderate]

- id: ugc-ad-library-creator-handle-ads
- fits: DTC brands and consumer apps spending, or ready to spend, at least $1k/month on Meta or TikTok ads. Also suits brands with seeding output (plays 1–2) they can license.
- why it works: Paid creative fatigues in weeks. Buying UGC at about $150–$200 per video is cheaper than any studio and produces many hooks to test. Running the best ones from the *creator's* handle (TikTok Spark Ads, Meta Partnership Ads) adds social proof. TikTok-reported and agency data point to higher engagement and lower CPA than brand-handle ads.
- steps:
  1. Write a brief with 5 hook angles: problem-first, "I was skeptical", side-by-side comparison, ASMR or unboxing, and founder story.
  2. Order 8–12 videos from 4–6 creators (marketplaces like Billo or Insense, or seeded creators from play 1). Ask for 3 hook variants per video and raw footage.
  3. Negotiate paid-usage rights for 6–12 months at the start.
  4. Launch as creator-handle ads: Spark codes on TikTok, Partnership Ads on Meta. Run brand-handle versions of the same video as the control.
  5. Kill losers after about 2× target CPA spent. Re-cut winners with new hooks.
  6. Refresh 4–6 new videos every month.
- cost & effort: $150–$200 per video median, with entry-level creators at $50–$100 before licensing ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/ugc-rates/), [Influee](https://influee.co/blog/ugc-price)). Paid-ads rights cost about $150–$300 more per year as an anchor ([Idukki](https://idukki.io/blog/ugc-creator-rate-card-what-to-charge.md)). Bundles of 5+ get about a 19% discount ([Influee](https://influee.co/blog/ugc-price)).
- time to first result: 2–3 weeks to delivered videos, then 7–14 days of ad testing.
- expected results:
  - TikTok-reported figures for Spark versus non-Spark ads: 142% higher engagement, 43% higher conversion rate and 4% lower CPM. These are platform self-reported numbers.
  - An agency reports 67% lower CPA versus standard TikTok ads across its clients (unverified). One retailer saw 25% higher CTR and 24% higher CVR ([Attn Agency](https://www.attnagency.com/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-whitelisting-guide), [StackInfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/tiktok-spark-ads-101-guide-for-sellers-creators/)).
  - Planning assumption: 1 in 5 to 1 in 10 UGC videos becomes a scalable winner (practitioner estimate).
- leading indicators: 3-second hook rate, thumb-stop rate, CTR, CPA by hook angle and creative half-life.
- risks & compliance: Paid usage without a written licence is copyright infringement. Creator-handle ads must keep "Paid partnership" labelling. Before/after claims in beauty and health need substantiation. Usage-rights expiry needs tracking.
- ClimbOS can automate:
  - ClimbOS: write briefs and hook scripts, shortlist UGC creators, draft licence terms, tag every asset with its rights expiry, analyse ad performance by hook and generate re-cut scripts.
  - Owner: approve spend, sign licences and authorise Spark or Partnership access.

## 6. Paid newsletter recommendations (Boosts / SparkLoop) [evidence: moderate]

- id: paid-newsletter-recommendations
- fits: Creators, course and coaching businesses and consumer apps whose funnel starts with a newsletter, especially if they already have a lead magnet. Needs an email-to-customer conversion you know, or can estimate, so a cost-per-subscriber cap can be set.
- why it works: Other newsletters recommend yours at signup or inside their sends, and you pay only per subscriber. On beehiiv, unengaged subscribers are refunded after a probation period. That makes it one of the few acquisition channels with a fixed, known unit cost.
- steps:
  1. Compute the maximum cost per subscriber you can pay: (subscriber → customer rate) × (first-year gross margin per customer) × 0.5.
  2. Create an offer on beehiiv Boosts (or SparkLoop's partner network if you're on another ESP). Write a one-sentence promise and a 3-line description aimed at a niche.
  3. Approve only publishers whose audience matches, and reject generalist "make money" lists.
  4. Watch 30-day engagement of boosted cohorts against organic ones, and pause publishers whose cohorts don't open.
  5. Put boosted subscribers into a welcome sequence that sells within 7 days.
  6. Also earn from the other side: recommend aligned newsletters to monetise your own signups.
- cost & effort: $1.50–$3.00 per subscriber typical, with beehiiv citing a $1.63 early-access average ([beehiiv Boosts](https://www.beehiiv.com/features/boosts), [roo.beehiiv](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-make-money-on-beehiiv)). About 2 hours to set up.
- time to first result: Days.
- expected results:
  - One creator's reported cost was about $2.00 per quality subscriber ([beehiiv experts' guide](https://blog.beehiiv.com/p/experts-guide-boosts)).

## 9. Subscribe-and-save replenishment [evidence: moderate]

- id: subscribe-and-save-replenishment
- fits: Consumables DTC (coffee, tea, snacks, supplements, pet food, skincare, cleaning refills, candles) with a natural use-up cycle of 2–12 weeks.
- why it works: Replenishment categories retain extremely well: 92–96% of first-box buyers take a second box in pet food, supplements and coffee. Turning one-time buyers into subscribers multiplies LTV, which raises the CPA every other play can afford.
- steps:
  1. Measure the median days-to-reorder per SKU from order history.
  2. Default the subscription frequency to that median and offer one step shorter and longer.
  3. Offer a 10–15% subscription discount plus one non-price perk (free shipping, early access or a free sample of a new flavour).
  4. Show the subscribe toggle *pre-selected* on PDPs for replenishable SKUs, A/B tested against unselected. Make it clearly visible to stay compliant (see risks).
  5. Send a "running low?" email to one-time buyers at 80% of the median reorder time, offering to convert to a subscription.
  6. Build easy skip, swap and pause flows to reduce cancellations, and run a cancellation survey.
- cost & effort: A subscription app at $0–$300/month (Recharge, Skio, Shopify Subscriptions, Loop). About 6–10 hours to set up.
- time to first result: 30–60 days, after one reorder cycle.
- expected results:
  - First-box to second-box retention of 92–96% in replenishment categories versus 30–40% loss in beauty curation boxes ([Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-subscription-trial-to-paid-conversion-by-vertical-2026)).
  - Monthly churn of about 6.5–7.1% for consumer subscriptions ([Eightx churn index](https://eightx.co/blog/dtc-subscription-churn-index-2026)).
  - Subscription checkout adoption of 10–30% of orders on replenishable SKUs is a typical target (practitioner estimate; Recharge defines the metric but no benchmark was found ([Recharge](https://getrecharge.com/?p=25583))).
- leading indicators: Subscription checkout rate, skip versus cancel ratio, and the conversion rate of the "running low" email.
- risks & compliance: Negative-option and auto-renewal laws require clear disclosure, express consent and easy cancellation. The FTC's Negative Option Rule, which includes click-to-cancel, was vacated by a federal court in July 2025, but ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws such as California's still apply. Have counsel check before pre-selecting the toggle.

  1. Map 5–10 subreddits and Discords where your buyers talk, and read the rules on self-promotion for each.
  2. Founder account, posting under real identity: answer 3–5 questions a week with genuinely useful answers, and disclose affiliation whenever your product is relevant.
  3. Once trusted, post a "we're building X, roast our prototype" or a behind-the-scenes post where allowed. Offer beta access or free samples to commenters.
  4. Host an AMA where the moderators allow it, coordinated with the mods in advance.
  5. Track brand-mention threads, and answer every question about your product.
  6. For apps: run your own Discord as the beta and feedback home, and invite power users as moderators.
- cost & effort: $0. 2–4 owner hours a week, which can't be fully delegated because authenticity matters.
- time to first result: 1–3 months.
- expected results: Small-brand outcome data doesn't exist publicly. One review of a Reddit agency found only 8 natural mentions in 4 weeks ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/best-reddit-marketing-agency-2026-i-hired-10-agencies-and-3-understood-how-reddit-actually-works-27305ad351)). Mark as weak evidence, because results are lumpy and one great thread can drive thousands of visits.
- leading indicators: Karma, accepted answers, organic mentions of the brand and referral traffic from reddit.com.
- risks & compliance: Undisclosed promotion and sockpuppets violate Reddit rules and the FTC rules (insider reviews must be disclosed), and get bans. Never buy upvotes, which is covered by the fake-indicators ban in the FTC 2024 rule.
- ClimbOS can automate:

  - Owner: post personally. ClimbOS should never auto-post on Reddit.

## 39. Live shopping (TikTok LIVE, Whatnot) [evidence: weak]

- id: live-shopping
- fits: Apparel (try-ons), beauty (demos), vintage and collectibles, candles and crafts made live, and food tastings. Needs a host comfortable on camera for several hours a week.
- why it works: Live demos answer objections in real time and create urgency. Vendor-cited conversion is 9–30% versus 2–3% for standard ecommerce, but that figure is vendor-sourced. Only sellers who stream regularly see the economics work.
- steps:
  1. Pick a fixed weekly schedule (e.g. Tue and Thu 7pm), because consistency drives the algorithm and returning viewers.
  2. Prepare a run-of-show: 3 hero products, a live-only bundle and time-boxed offers.
  3. Bring a co-host or moderator to handle comments and pin products.
  4. Promote each live beforehand on short-form video.
  5. Clip the best moments into short videos after each live.
  6. Track revenue per live-hour, and quit after 8 weeks if it is below your hourly floor.
- cost & effort: 6–25 hours a week. TikTok Shop fees, or about 10% on Whatnot ([Awisee](https://awisee.com/blog/best-live-shopping-platforms/)).
- time to first result: 2–8 weeks.
- expected results: Live conversion of 9–30% is vendor-cited ([eMarketer citing Firework](https://www.emarketer.com/content/faq-on-livestream-commerce--what-marketers-need-know-about-live-shopping-2026)). A weakly sourced figure puts average TikTok LIVE CVR at 5–12%, with successful sellers streaming 15–25 hours a week ([Dashboardly](https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-live-shopping-statistics)). Only 11% of creator-driven shoppers report buying from a livestream (eMarketer/impact.com). The livestream share of TikTok Shop sales fell from 26% to 18% between July 2024 and July 2025 ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/marketplace-briefing-tiktok-shop-and-whatnot-notch-record-breaking-black-friday-cyber-monday-sales-as-live-shopping-surges/)).
- leading indicators: Average concurrent viewers, the percentage of viewers clicking products and revenue per live-hour.
- risks & compliance: Live claims about health benefits, and pricing claims ("was $X") must be accurate. Live-only scarcity must be real.

## 40. Verified impact angle [evidence: moderate (market), weak (per-brand lift)]

- id: verified-impact-angle
- fits: Food, home, beauty and apparel brands with a *real*, documentable sustainability or social attribute: refill systems, certified materials, local sourcing, a measurable donation model, B Corp or certifications.
- why it works: In the NYU Stern and Circana index, sustainability-marketed products held 23.8% of US CPG share in 2024 but delivered about 41% of CPG market growth over 2013–2024. The 2025 edition reports 25.4% share and 44.9% of growth. A specific, verified claim also gives editors, retailers (Faire boutiques, co-ops) and corporate gift buyers a reason to choose you. It is a multiplier on plays 8, 21, 34 and 37.
- steps:
  1. Audit what is actually true and measurable (e.g. "every refill saves 38 g of plastic, measured by our supplier's spec").
  2. Obtain third-party proof where possible (certification, supplier documents, an LCA summary).
  3. Write claims that are specific and qualified, never vague ("eco-friendly", "green").
  4. Put an impact counter on the site and in post-purchase emails ("you've saved X").
  5. Add the angle to gift-guide pitches, Faire listings and corporate gifting tiers.
  6. Publish an annual impact report, which doubles as a press hook.
- cost & effort: $0–$5k depending on certification. 6–15 hours.
- time to first result: 1–3 months, often through other plays.
- expected results: Market-level evidence: the sustainability segment grew faster than conventional ([NYU Stern SMSI 2024](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/2025-04/SMSI%202024%20Accessible.pdf), [NYU Stern index page](https://stern.nyu.edu/experience-stern/about/departments-centers-initiatives/centers-of-research/center-sustainable-business/research/csb-sustainable-market-share-index)). Price premiums have stabilised since their 2018 peak ([Circana](https://www.circana.com/post/u-s-positioned-for-additional-growth-as-sustainability-marketed-products-flourish-in-europe)). There is no per-brand conversion-lift data, so treat it as a positioning multiplier, not a standalone channel.
- leading indicators: Impact-claim mentions in reviews, press pickup and retailer inquiries citing the attribute.
- risks & compliance: The FTC Green Guides (16 CFR 260) require substantiation and prohibit unqualified general environmental benefit claims. The EU Green Claims and Empowering Consumers rules are stricter. Greenwashing claims attract class actions.
- ClimbOS can automate:
  - ClimbOS: run the claims audit checklist, flag claims against the Green Guides, write specific claim language, build the impact counter and report and weave the angle into pitches.
  - Owner: provide the evidence and approve the claims (legal review recommended).

## How the engine should combine plays (sequencing notes)

Several plays are force multipliers and should be sequenced, not run in isolation:

- **Reviews first.** Play 3 (or play 22 on Amazon) precedes paid amplification and most creator plays. Ads and creators sent to a page with zero reviews waste traffic.
- **Affiliate infrastructure before outreach.** Play 17 is a prerequisite for plays 1, 8 and 18. Editors and YouTubers expect a link that pays.
- **The seeding → UGC → ads loop.** Plays 1 and 2 generate content, and play 5 licenses the winners and runs them from creator handles. That is the closest thing to a compounding machine for small DTC brands.
- **The creator business stack.** Play 6 (paid recommendations) and play 14 (JV partners) fill play 15 or 16 (challenge or webinar). Play 36 (BookBub) feeds the same funnel for authors.
- **The app stack.** Plays 12 and 13 raise conversion of every install source. Play 31 (recap cards) and play 30 (referral) add organic inflow. Play 32 (featuring) is a free lottery ticket submitted with every release.
- **Wholesale ladder.** Play 21 (Faire) builds the velocity proof that play 37 (retail open call) requires. Play 40 (impact angle) helps both.
